# Abhiara Foundation — Global Nonprofit Platform Addendum

**Purpose.** This addendum incorporates three further official reference families—**The Antara Foundation (TAF)**, **UNICEF**, and **Bal Raksha Bharat / Save the Children India**—into the existing platform plan. It extracts practical information, payment, safeguarding and reporting disciplines that a small organisation can operate **now**. It is **not** a brief to copy a reference organisation’s words, brand, images, stories, scale claims, fundraising products or governance claims.

**Reviewed:** 26 September 2026  
**Canonical public domain:** **`https://www.abhiarafoundation.org`**

> **Decision in one sentence:** Make `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org` a small, bilingual, education-first public record: one clear current purpose, one-time-only giving, dated and privacy-safe updates, plainly limited governance/finance information, and a responsible owner who can correct or unpublish material quickly.

## 1. Non-negotiables retained by this addendum

These conditions override any pattern observed on the reference sites.

| Area | Required public and operational rule |
|---|---|
| **Official domain** | Use **only** `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org` in canonical tags, payment instructions, QR instructions, sharing text, emails, social metadata and public links. Redirect the non-`www` domain to the equivalent `www` URL. Do not promote another domain, a personal payment link or an unsolicited payment instruction. |
| **Programme scope** | **Abhiara Shiksha Sathi** is the sole current primary programme. Limited verified support is secondary, capacity-bound and clearly labelled. Historical records are not current delivery. **Abhiara Vidyapitha** is a future vision only—not an operating elder home, active programme, service, referral route or fundraising promise. |
| **Giving model** | **One-time payment only.** No monthly/recurring toggle, subscription, mandate, instalment, auto-renewal, renewal reminder, post-payment recurring ask or committed-giving language. A *Monthly Report* is a reporting format, never a monthly-giving product. |
| **80G and acknowledgement** | **80G approval is pending. Current contributions are not eligible for an 80G tax deduction.** Do not use an 80G badge, tax-saving language, “tax receipt” or “80G receipt.” A **Donation Acknowledgement** may confirm receipt of payment; it is not an 80G certificate or tax receipt. |
| **Donors and payment privacy** | No donor wall, public donor names, amounts, transaction references, testimonials, dashboards, sponsorship, donor-to-child contact or individual gift tracking. Collect only information required for payment, acknowledgement/compliance and a donor’s query. Keep card/bank credentials with the authorised processor, not the site or CMS. Marketing consent is a separate, unticked opt-in. |
| **Child safety and privacy** | Default to text-led, non-identifying content. Do not publish a child’s name, face, voice, school/class plus locality, precise location/timetable, academic record, family circumstance, health/trauma information, aid amount, route, contact detail or a re-identifying combination of facts. Do not use emotionally intense child imagery to persuade a donation. |
| **Media exception** | Before exceptional child-related media is published, require documented guardian consent and age-appropriate child assent where applicable, plus a separate safeguarding/privacy, rights, metadata/geolocation and dignity review. Consent does not remove the need to withhold risky material. A text-only update is always acceptable. |
| **Claim discipline** | No scale theatre, large cumulative reach, broad geography maps, “lives changed,” unsupported outcomes, partner logos or security badges without evidence/permission. Every public number needs a definition, period, safe geography, source, accountable reviewer and last-reviewed date. |
| **Language** | Essential English and Odia content must have equivalent meaning and human review, particularly payment, tax, privacy, consent, safeguarding, eligibility and complaints material. Never rely on unreviewed machine translation for those subjects. |

The three references demonstrate that trust can be distributed across purpose, current work, governance, policy, reporting and contact routes. For Abhiara, this should mean **a few maintained, inspectable pages**, not an imitation of a global or nationwide institution.[^taf-home][^unicef-home][^brb-campaign]

## 2. What is worth adapting now—and why

### A. A short path from purpose to proof

TAF’s useful pattern is an inspectable narrative: purpose, how work is done, who is accountable and where records can be checked. UNICEF makes safeguarding, reporting and transparency continuously findable. Bal Raksha Bharat places payment terms, policy routes and organisational information near a focused donation action.[^taf-work][^unicef-safe][^brb-donate]

**Abhiara adaptation:** on every important page, give a visitor a small next step that can genuinely be delivered:

1. **Explore current education work**;
2. **Read a dated verified update**; or
3. **Make a one-time contribution**.

A footer can additionally offer **Board & Transparency**, **Safety & concerns**, **Privacy**, **Contact**, and **Policies**. Do not add recruitment, fellowship, newsletter, volunteer, petition, community-reporting, referral or fundraising paths unless there is a named owner, a response standard, screening capacity and an actual process behind each one.

### B. Public accountability that matches real capacity

TAF’s finance/legal page and annual-report archive make mature documentation easy to locate. UNICEF’s public accountability links and Bal Raksha Bharat’s separate governance, financial, safeguarding and policy destinations show the value of clear pathways.[^taf-finance][^taf-report][^unicef-results][^brb-financials]

**Abhiara adaptation:** publish a maintained **Board & Transparency** page and a compact **Documents & Updates** list rather than an empty research hub, data portal, annual-report archive or dashboard. A truthful “not yet available” status is better than a generic badge or a placeholder download.

### C. Safeguarding visible as an operating commitment

UNICEF’s official safeguarding page describes risk reduction from design through delivery and applies expected practice to staff, partners, suppliers and vendors; it links directly to a concern route.[^unicef-safe] Bal Raksha Bharat presents awareness, prevention, reporting and response as core safeguarding areas, and makes its scope and emergency signposting visible.[^brb-safe]

**Abhiara adaptation:** before seeking child stories, children’s images, volunteers or child-centred donations, publish a short bilingual **Child Safeguarding & Privacy** page that says what Abhiara can genuinely do now. It should cover:

- expected conduct and prohibited behaviour for directors, staff, volunteers, vendors, photographers and delivery partners;
- safer selection/reference checks, induction, supervision and escalation appropriate to actual roles;
- photography/recording, consent, assent, retention, withdrawal and takedown rules;
- data minimisation and a strict no-identifying-child-content default;
- a monitored concern route, the **role** responsible for safeguarding, acknowledgement expectations, scope and emergency direction; and
- effective date, approving role, last-reviewed date and next review date.

Keep a safety concern route separate from the ordinary contact/feedback form. Do not request detailed case material through an unsecured general form, promise anonymous investigation or absolute confidentiality if Abhiara cannot deliver it, or claim an independent investigations service, nationwide focal-point network or 24/7 response capacity that does not exist.

### D. Evidence before impact language

TAF’s reports show the value of naming a reporting period, source and methodological detail when a result is presented. UNICEF’s annual reporting and transparency portal distinguish reported data from provisional information. Bal Raksha Bharat’s record archive is useful in principle, but its differing public scale figures are a reminder to control every public number at source.[^taf-report][^unicef-report][^unicef-open][^brb-reports]

**Abhiara adaptation:** use an evidence ladder rather than an impact dashboard:

| Evidence level | Suitable public wording | What must exist before publication |
|---|---|---|
| **Intention** | “We plan to…” / “We are exploring…” | Dated plan, dependency and accountable role. |
| **Documented activity** | “We held…” / “We shared…” / “We supported…” | Dated activity log, purchase/partner record or equivalent. |
| **Immediate output** | “Three sessions were delivered…” | Definition, period, source owner and duplicate check. |
| **Voluntary feedback** | “Among voluntary respondents…” | Method, response count, period, limitation and privacy decision. |
| **Learning/outcome** | “Early monitoring suggests…” | Suitable measure, timeframe, method, limitation and preferably independent review before a strong claim. |

Attendance, materials purchased, social-media views or a donation total are **not educational outcomes**. Where evidence is early or incomplete, say what happened and what will be checked next instead of manufacturing a percentage or a beneficiary total.

## 3. Specific page improvements

### 3.1 Homepage — education first, current evidence second, trust always visible

**Objective:** within one screen and a short scroll, a first-time visitor should understand the present education purpose, identify the latest verified information, see a safe action and locate accountability material.

| Order | Recommended element | Implement now | Must not do |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Calm header** | Use the English/Odia control, simple navigation and a single **Donate once** action. Each public action should resolve to `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/...`. | Do not use multiple competing donation buttons, a monthly-support label, a non-`www` destination or payment URL hidden behind an image. |
| 2 | **Education-first hero** | State the present purpose in plain language: education continuity, dignity and careful support through **Abhiara Shiksha Sathi**. Link first to current education work; offer a secondary text link to one-time giving. | Do not lead with broad multi-sector claims, elder-care operations, national reach, a child’s image or an unverified impact total. |
| 3 | **What is happening now** | Show one current, owner-approved activity statement with a last-verified date, safe broad geography only where approved, and a **What can be verified** link. | Do not turn historical education activity into a current monthly result or infer learning impact from participation. |
| 4 | **Latest verified update** | Use one dated card: activity/period, narrow verified output if available, source status, limitation and next review. Label its content type (for example, *Field update* or *Governance update*). | Do not use a sentimental beneficiary story, a press clipping without rights review or an unlabelled “impact” card. |
| 5 | **Purpose carried forward** | Include the short organisation-level founder-purpose treatment in §3.2. | Do not add a founder portrait, private biography, personal home/location history, family detail or quotation as proof of programme impact. |
| 6 | **Birthday with Purpose** | Use one quiet card linking to the bounded page in §3.3. | Do not show birthdays, names, campaign totals, targets, e-cards, registries, reminders or social proof. |
| 7 | **Trust line** | Place a factual line next to a link: **“One-time contribution only · 80G approval is pending · Read how we protect privacy and manage donations.”** | Do not show tax, security, audit, partner or award badges unless their exact basis is verified and published. |
| 8 | **Accountability footer** | Make **Board & Transparency**, **Safety & concerns**, **Privacy**, **Policies**, **Updates** and **Contact** permanently findable. | Do not hide safeguarding and correction routes in a long legal footer or make donation the only meaningful action. |

This is the small-organisation version of the references’ purpose → work → evidence → accountability journey, not their scale-led homepage model.[^taf-home][^unicef-home][^brb-campaign]

### 3.2 Founder-purpose story — restore warmth without publicising private life

**Recommended page/location:** a short **Our story / A purpose carried forward** block on the homepage, with an optional short page at `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/our-story`.

**Use this editorial structure:**

1. **Why the organisation begins with education.** Speak in an organisation voice about steady learning, dignity and everyday hope.
2. **How the organisation chooses to act.** Say that work is carefully verified, limited by capacity and reported plainly.
3. **What the organisation will not claim.** Distinguish current education work, limited verified support, historical records and future plans.
4. **Where accountability sits.** Link to Board & Transparency and the safeguarding/privacy page.

**Safe starter copy for owner review**

> **A purpose carried forward**  
> Abhiara Foundation exists to stand carefully beside learning, dignity and everyday hope. We begin with education because steady learning can help children continue with confidence. We will speak plainly about what is happening now, what is still only a plan and where support is limited.

The references show that defined institutional and delivery roles can add credibility; Abhiara should use that lesson on **Board & Transparency**, not turn a founder into a fundraising device.[^taf-people][^unicef-who][^brb-governance]

**Keep separate:** a legally necessary director/officer disclosure, if verified and appropriate to publish, belongs in the Board & Transparency record with role, appointment period, responsibility and conflict-of-interest route. Do not create a personal founder narrative, personal contact channel, family story, image gallery, private address/location trail, qualifications showcase, child/founder anecdote or a claim that a founder’s history proves impact.

### 3.3 Birthday with Purpose — an editorial invitation, not a fundraising product

**Recommended public URL:** `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/birthday-with-purpose` (or a canonical redirect to the equivalent retained URL). Its single payment action must point to **`https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/donate`**.

**Purpose:** allow a visitor to mark a birthday with a one-time contribution to education without collecting occasion data or creating peer-to-peer fundraising.

**Required page blocks**

1. A two- to three-sentence invitation: *“If you wish, mark a birthday with a one-time gift for education.”*
2. One button: **Make a one-time donation for education**.
3. Optional copyable private sharing text, in reviewed English and Odia, containing only the canonical donation link: `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/donate`.
4. A concise assurance: **no birthday registry, public donor list, personal campaign page, fundraising target, child sponsorship or recurring donation**.
5. Links to the donation notice, privacy policy, safeguarding page and current verified updates.

**Remove or do not introduce:** birthday/occasion registration, date of birth, recipient or honouree name, guest list, e-card, messages, social handles, photographs, annual reminders, goal meters, peer-to-peer pages, fundraiser codes, celebration gallery, memorial/occasion checkout, donor recognition or a gift-in-honour workflow. The donation and child-protection references can support a clear action and a local payment disclosure; they do **not** justify a recurring campaign, child-linked occasion product or public donor story.[^unicef-donate][^brb-donate]

### 3.4 Donation page — one decision, clear terms before payment

**Recommended canonical URL:** `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/donate`.

Use a compact page: short education proposition → one one-time payment panel → concise alternative payment information only if current and verified → short FAQ → policy/contact footer. The panel should use three or four optional suggested amounts and **Other amount**, not item-by-item promises. State what general education support *can help* make possible and include a truthful flexible-allocation note if funds are not restricted.

#### Required copy and decisions next to the amount/payment control

1. **Exact giving-model notice:**

   > **One-time donation only. No recurring charge, subscription, mandate, instalment or automatic renewal is created.**

2. **Exact tax-status meaning:**

   > **80G approval is pending. Current contributions are not eligible for an 80G tax deduction.**

3. **Acknowledgement clarification:**

   > **A Donation Acknowledgement may be requested. It confirms that a donation has been received; it is not an 80G certificate or tax receipt.**

4. **Official-channel warning:**

   > **Donate only through `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org`, the published Foundation account or the authorised payment channel shown there. Abhiara Foundation will not change payment details through an unsolicited message.**

5. **Before checkout, disclose only verified facts:** legal payee/entity name; Section 8 status **when confirmed**; authorised processor; privacy link; what donor information is used for; acknowledgement timing; contact/correction/refund route; and the current allocation approach.

6. **Collect only necessary data:** payment/receipt-required identity and contact data, statutory fields if actually required, and separate unticked consent for optional updates. Never require marketing permission to give.

7. **Confirmation:** show the donor only their reference, amount, date, legal entity, acknowledgement expectation, help/correction route and link to current education updates. Do not promise a named child, a specific item, an education outcome, a private report, public recognition or a tax receipt.

The useful reference pattern is transparent information at the decision point: UNICEF visibly differentiates a single gift from its recurring programme, while Bal Raksha Bharat presents payment/support information, FAQs and allocation explanation near its giving route.[^unicef-donate][^brb-donate] Abhiara must retain **only** the explanatory discipline, not recurring controls, 80G claims or tax data collection designed for a different legal status.

**Private operating control:** reconcile payment attempts, successful payments, failures, donation acknowledgements, corrections/refunds and support queries at least monthly in a restricted owner-managed ledger. Do not publish transaction records, donor names or donation totals that cannot be reconciled.

### 3.5 Board & Transparency — one maintainable public record

**Recommended public URL:** `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/board-and-transparency`.

Instead of several thin trust pages, use one dated page with five short sections:

| Section | Publish only after verification | Owner action |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal identity and contact** | Legal entity name, incorporation/registration facts, registered office/contact and applicable statutory identifiers. State a status as “pending,” “not applicable” or “not yet publicly available” where that is the truth. | Record source document, fact owner, publication date and next review. |
| **People and responsibilities** | Only actual directors, formally engaged advisors, education owner/lead, safeguarding lead role and finance/contact role. Use a short responsibility statement, appointment month/year and one non-sensitive verified context line. | Do not publish decorative biographies, personal phone numbers or unconfirmed affiliations. Record conflict-of-interest declaration/policy link. |
| **Policies and concerns** | Child Safeguarding & Privacy; code of conduct if adopted; donation/refund/correction terms; concern route; effective/review dates. | Test the monitored route periodically and show an honest response expectation. |
| **Documents and finance status** | Annual activity/stewardship note, finance summary, audited/statutory documents and filings only when real, approved and safe to share. | Label document type, period, source, issue date, publication date, last review and superseded/archive state. |
| **Corrections and limits** | A corrections email/contact route; explanation of how material errors are dated and corrected; clear statement of what is not yet available. | Append a dated correction note rather than silently rewriting a material claim. |

TAF’s audited financials by financial year are a valuable discoverability pattern, and UNICEF/Bal Raksha Bharat show why governance, safeguarding and reporting routes should not be hidden.[^taf-finance][^unicef-home][^brb-financials] Abhiara should not simulate their maturity with empty audit pages, made-up independent oversight, partner-logo grids, FCRA claims, evaluator claims or a long list of advisors.

### 3.6 Monthly Reports — short, evidence-led updates, not an impact feed

**Recommended public URL:** `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/updates` with a **Monthly Reports** section only when there is a genuinely verified report for that month. The name should never imply monthly donations.

A small organisation does not need to publish every month. Use the following decision rule:

- **If there is a verified activity record:** publish one short monthly update.
- **If there was no reportable activity:** publish nothing, or publish a candid operational note only if there is a material public reason.
- **If data is still being checked:** label the item **In review** internally; do not present it as public impact.
- **For historic records:** use the controlled label **Historical education archive — not a current monthly delivery metric** and keep them below current reporting.

**Required monthly update template**

| Field | Public treatment |
|---|---|
| **Status and content type** | *Current education work*; *Field update*; *Governance update*; or a controlled historical label. |
| **Period and safe geography** | Month/period and approved broad geography only. |
| **What happened** | A factual, bounded activity statement. |
| **What Abhiara did** | A clear description of Abhiara’s contribution, separated from any partner’s contribution. |
| **What can be verified** | Narrow output only, with definition, source owner and provisional/final status. |
| **Learning or limitation** | One honest learning, constraint or data gap. Do not turn attendance or supplies into learning outcomes. |
| **Privacy note** | State that identifying child/household information has been removed; do not publish the underlying sensitive record. |
| **Accountability** | Publication date, last-verified date, reviewer role, next review date and correction/removal route. |

At the end of a complete reporting period, convert the verified updates into a short **annual activity and transparency note**: work attempted/completed, learning and changes, modest funds-use summary when reliable, policy/reporting status and no child personal data. This is the early-stage counterpart to the references’ fiscal-year reporting—not a global dashboard, a large annual-impact book or an external-evaluation claim.[^taf-report][^unicef-report][^brb-reports]

### 3.7 No-code owner controls — a publishing gate, not a large platform

Use a small role-restricted CMS/dashboard. It should manage controlled publication; it must not become a public impact portal, a child-data database, a donation-record viewer or a safeguarding case-management system.

#### Public content types

| Content type | Minimum controlled fields |
|---|---|
| **Page** | English text; reviewed Odia text; content owner; publication date; last/next review; public status; correction history. |
| **Programme/update** | Controlled status; activity date/period; safe geography; intended audience; verified facts; limitation; metric definition; source/evidence reference; fact reviewer; public/archive state; English/Odia approvals; publish/review dates. |
| **Monthly report** | The update fields above plus report month, output counting method, provisional/final status, correction note and archive state. |
| **Document/policy** | Type; effective/reporting period; source; issue/expiry; approver; publication/review dates; public/private setting; English/Odia availability; superseded-by link; archive flag. |
| **Person/role** | Approved public name where appropriate; role; statutory/advisory/operating classification; appointment period; responsibility; verified public context line; conflict/policy link; review date. |
| **Donation notice/site setting** | Canonical domain (`https://www.abhiarafoundation.org`); verified legal payee and processor display; one-time-only and 80G-pending copy version; official contact; acknowledgement expectation; policy links; last-verified date. |

#### Private, access-restricted registers

- **Donation ledger:** payment reference, amount, date, receipt-essential contact data, optional communications consent, payment/acknowledgement/reconciliation/correction status. Do not store card or bank credentials, child data or programme case details here.
- **Safeguarding, consent and media-review register:** consent/assent reference where applicable, risk/minimisation decision, rights/asset decision, reviewer, expiry/removal request and publication link. Keep complaint case files and attachments in separate restricted systems accessible only to authorised safeguarding roles.
- **Evidence register:** source record, source owner, date, definition, duplicate-control decision, limitations and public-claim link. This prevents a social post, photo or unverified message from becoming an “impact” number.

#### Small, practical permissions and workflow

| Role | Can do | Cannot do alone |
|---|---|---|
| **Content owner** | Draft/update pages and attach evidence references. | Publish numerical, payment, legal, policy or child-related content. |
| **Programme/fact reviewer** | Verify activity details, count definition, period and source. | Clear safeguarding/media or change payment/legal copy. |
| **Safeguarding/privacy reviewer** | Approve/decline public story/media and set review/expiry/removal conditions. | Approve factual evidence outside their remit. |
| **Bilingual reviewer** | Confirm equivalent English/Odia meaning for essential content. | Substitute unreviewed translation. |
| **Director/policy owner** | Approve policy, governance, payment and high-risk public changes. | Bypass factual or safeguarding review. |
| **CMS publisher** | Publish, check canonical links/mobile display and immediately unpublish. | Alter approved source/claim/payment content without a new review. |

**Mandatory pre-publication gate:** fact source → verification date and status label → evidence/number definition → privacy/safeguarding/media gate → rights decision → English/Odia equivalence → responsible approval → publication date and next review date. If one check is incomplete, publish a narrower text-only version or keep it internal.

**Immediate unpublish controls:** the CMS owner must be able to unpublish an item promptly for a privacy/safeguarding concern, factual error, payment issue, expired consent, broken official payment route or an unreviewed translation. Preserve a restricted change log; material public corrections receive a dated correction note.

## 4. What not to copy from the references

| Do not import | Why it is unsuitable for Abhiara now |
|---|---|
| Global/national scale, large reach figures, maps, dense partner logos, government-policy influence or a broad programme menu | These reflect mature operating/reporting systems and would overstate Abhiara’s current education-first scope. |
| UNICEF-style recurring giving, automatic debit, monthly defaults, conversion/retention prompts or Bal Raksha Bharat’s committed-gift mechanics | Abhiara accepts one-time payments only. |
| Any 80G/tax benefit, tax receipt, tax calculator or eligibility statement | **80G approval is pending**; current contributions are not eligible for an 80G deduction. |
| Identifiable child images, child sponsorship, named beneficiary profiles, family adversity narratives or donor-to-child messaging | They conflict with Abhiara’s strict child privacy, dignity and safeguarding position. |
| Donor walls, public donor testimonials/amounts, leaderboards, social-proof pressure or celebration galleries | Abhiara has a no-donor-wall rule and does not need public recognition to build trust. |
| Complex transparency dashboards, interactive maps, research hubs, evaluation portals or investigation systems | They would create an unreliable maintenance burden and imply capacity that has not been established. |
| Broad marketing consent, default WhatsApp/SMS/email outreach or donor-data sharing beyond payment/acknowledgement needs | Marketing is optional, separately unticked and subject to data minimisation. |
| Fellowship, recruiting, volunteer or newsletter funnels without named owners, response standards, screening and safeguarding procedure | An unstaffed pathway creates an unmet expectation and can introduce safety risk. |
| External institutions’ wording, visual identity, photos, stories, awards, financial figures, case studies or endorsements | The addendum adapts accountability principles only; it does not license or validate copying another organisation’s materials. |

## 5. Practical 90-day sequence

### First 30 days — make safety and payment truth visible

1. Enforce the canonical **`https://www.abhiarafoundation.org`** domain on public links, payment instructions, metadata, QR instructions and redirects.
2. Remove or disable recurring/monthly-support controls, celebration registries, donor-wall features, public donor data, unsupported counters and child-identifying media.
3. Publish/approve the short bilingual Child Safeguarding & Privacy page, a separate concern route, privacy notice and payment/donation notice.
4. Verify the legal payee, authorised processor, official contact, one-time checkout behaviour, 80G-pending wording, acknowledgement wording and refund/correction route before public payment promotion.
5. Create restricted donation, evidence and consent/media registers; assign the actual responsible roles.

### Days 31–60 — build the small public foundation

1. Simplify the homepage using §3.1; lead with current education work and one latest verified update.
2. Publish the organisation-level founder-purpose copy and short Birthday with Purpose editorial page.
3. Establish Board & Transparency with verified legal/status information, people/roles, policies, documents/status and correction contact.
4. Configure the required CMS fields, publishing gate, bilingual review and immediate-unpublish control.

### Days 61–90 — begin consistent, modest reporting

1. Publish one current education update only after it clears the evidence and privacy gate.
2. Place reviewed historical material in a clearly labelled archive, never in current monthly reporting.
3. Reconcile donations, payment failures, acknowledgements and corrections monthly in the private ledger.
4. Test the concern route, payment hand-off, confirmation, canonical links, English/Odia equivalence and policy review dates.
5. At the end of a complete reporting period, prepare a short annual activity and transparency note only from reconciled evidence.
