# Abhiara Website Content v3 Adoption Note

The supplied content proposal was reviewed against the current website, verified public records, privacy rules and legal status.

## Built into the website

1. **Clear education support process**
   - Six plain steps from request to follow up.
   - Support is reviewed and is not automatic.
   - Tuition, books, supplies, examination needs and other checked learning needs are examples, not a fixed package.
   - Abhiara Shiksha Sathi is education support. It is not a residential child care home.

2. **Clear public reporting rules**
   - Public reports may show the period, broad place, support provided, a checked combined result and a reviewed amount when available.
   - Child names, exact addresses, school records, identity documents, bank papers, medical records, certificate numbers and private family history remain private.
   - An unchecked record or photo stays unpublished.

3. **Safer contact form**
   - Visitors are asked not to send private child documents through the contact form.
   - The fixed 48 hour response promise was removed.
   - Contact-page contrast was corrected.

4. **Approved founder and future-plan facts retained**
   - Raisar, Kendrapara, Odisha career and later Mumbai journey.
   - Future school, elder home, livelihood, digital learning and wellness plans remain clearly marked as future-only with no current enrolment.

## Not copied from the proposal

- Placeholder people, stories, addresses, registration numbers and staff names.
- Unverified child case stories or school identities.
- Unverified impact numbers or programme-scale claims.
- New donation causes that are not supported by the current payment and accounting model.
- A 24 or 48 hour response guarantee.
- Claims that future initiatives are already operating.
- Public medical records, certificate numbers, family hardship details or identity documents.

This approach uses the helpful structure from the proposal without weakening Abhiara Foundation's existing accuracy, privacy and safeguarding rules.
