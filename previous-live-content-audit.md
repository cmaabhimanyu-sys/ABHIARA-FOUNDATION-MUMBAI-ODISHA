## 1. Non-negotiable publication rules

These rules take precedence over legacy copy, assets, redirects, metadata, CMS defaults, and any prior page design.

1. **Official web identity:** Canonical URLs, structured data, sitemap, social/OG URLs, payment warnings, and public links must use **`https://www.abhiarafoundation.org`**. Do not reintroduce `.com` URLs or accept a social/WhatsApp message as an independently authoritative source of payment details.
2. **Education first:** Abhiara Shiksha Sathi is the only programme to describe as the primary active programme. Do not use historic one-off records to imply continuing delivery, capacity, geographic coverage, cumulative impact, or a broad current portfolio.
3. **Limited verified support is secondary:** Fire relief, water service, and the elder visit belong only in a dated **past verified support** archive, outside flagship navigation and marketing claims.
4. **Vidyapitha is a plan:** An old-age home/elder-dignity facility belongs only under **Abhiara Vidyapitha — future vision, not a current programme**. The 2025 elder-home visit is not evidence that Abhiara operates, funds, or plans an active elder-care service now.
5. **Privacy and safeguarding by default:** Do not publish a child’s or founder’s private details. Do not re-use a legacy photograph, video, caption, name, school, precise address, family circumstance, aid amount, or identifying case narrative merely because it survives in Git or storage.
6. **No donor exposure or misleading payment claims:** No donor wall, donor dashboard, public donor name/amount/reference, recurring plan, mandate, subscription, automatic renewal, or instalment product. Do not call a payment confirmation or acknowledgement a tax receipt. While 80G is pending, use only the approved **Donation Acknowledgement on request** language.
7. **No broad active-programme claims:** Do not surface legacy totals, fallback figures, unspecified “activities completed,” unverifiable `50+` counts, projected figures, or counts without an explicit period, denominator, record owner, and review status. A historical record may report its own narrow, verified aggregate.
8. **Evidence is not automatically publication-ready:** Repository presence, a static image, a CloudFront/manus-storage URL, an old blog, or a newspaper clipping is evidence of retention — **not** current consent, copyright permission, factual verification, or an approval to publish.

### Required record template

Every restored record must be a CMS-managed, independently reviewable object with these public or internal fields:

- **Public:** title; `Historical verified activity` or `Historical — outside current flagship programme` label; activity date (or month only where exact date is unresolved); appropriately generalised place; activity/support type; only verified aggregate; explicit limitation; public evidence citation/status; last content-review date.
- **Internal only:** source-file/hash or primary-record reference; fact verifier; approval owner; fact-review date; consent/safeguarding decision; media decision and expiry/review date; copyright/press decision; source and asset-retention status; redaction notes; redirect targets.
- **No public fields for:** beneficiary names; founder/personnel stories; individual student details; family circumstances; exact addresses; school/class/course identifiers unless independently approved; payment/audit details unrelated to the record; raw media URLs; aid amount; signatures; phone/email; or unredacted press scans without a rights decision.

A record must be published only after all mandatory internal fields are complete. A missing consent, rights, date, or verification decision means **publish text without that detail**, defer it, or do not publish the record.

## 6. Privacy, media, press, and people controls

### Media decision gate

Before any legacy image/video is public again, apply a **record-by-record** decision, not a blanket “grandfathering” rule:

1. Verify the depicted activity and caption against primary records.
2. Identify every recognisable child, elder, emergency-aid recipient, and other person where reasonably possible.
3. Confirm current documented consent for the exact public use; for children, confirm guardian consent plus safeguarding review. Prior public display is insufficient.
4. Assess dignity, vulnerability, re-identification, geolocation/metadata, context, and public-interest risk.
5. Confirm ownership/licence, external host retention controls, and takedown capability.
6. Approve, crop/redact, substitute a non-identifying documentary image, publish text only, or permanently withhold. Document the decision and reviewer.

Until this is complete, archive cards must be **text-only**. The current `OtherVerifiedSupport` rendering must not display arbitrary CMS `imageUrl` values; it needs the same consent-reviewed media control (or stronger) used for education. Legacy direct asset URLs and externally hosted videos need an inventory and access/takedown review, even if no route currently imports them.

### Press evidence

The seven legacy press clippings are potentially valuable provenance, especially six associated with Pratibha Samman and one with the August education activity. They are not a general press campaign.

- Store the originals and source metadata internally.
- For each publicly referenced item, record newspaper/publisher, publication date, page if known, URL or retained source, event link, copyright status, and public-display decision.
- Prefer citation/link or rights-cleared thumbnail to full clipping reproduction where full reuse is uncertain.
- Use an accessible “Evidence” disclosure/drawer on the relevant education record card rather than a standalone Media Room.
- Do not use a clipping to infer facts beyond what it actually reports; resolve discrepancies in favour of the primary verified record.

### Founder, board, and private details

The supplied people/organisation audit did not complete. The source review nevertheless shows founder-centric legacy/current material that conflicts with the mandatory no-private-founder-detail direction. Before retaining `/our-story` or biographies:

- remove/review founder photograph, name attribution beyond legally necessary governance disclosure, rural-to-city journey, personal/family history, qualifications, locations, and personal messages;
- remove claims that non-education support is currently delivered as a broad ongoing portfolio;
- do not use a founder narrative to substantiate field impact;
- separate any legally required director/officer disclosure from storytelling, and independently verify its necessity and accuracy.

The public rule is **organisation-level purpose, not personal biography**. The same principle applies to board, staff, volunteers, beneficiaries, and referral contacts.

## 7. Donor, financial, legal, and safeguarding restoration controls

The financial/Donor audit should restore transparency **discoverability**, not old content or assurances.

### Immediate payment and donor controls

At every payment call to action and beside UPI/QR/bank details, state in English and Odia with identical meaning:

- **One-time donation only. No recurring charge, subscription, mandate, instalment, or automatic renewal is created.**
- 80G approval is pending; current donations are not eligible for an 80G tax deduction.
- A **Donation Acknowledgement** may be requested; it is not represented as an 80G/tax receipt.
- Donate only through `www.abhiarafoundation.org`, the published Foundation account, or the authorised payment channel shown there; no cash, agent, or personal account.
- Abhiara will not change QR/bank details through an unsolicited message. Independently confirm any change through the published contact page / `info@abhiarafoundation.org`.

Keep payment collection to the approved one-time flow. Perform an owner/bank confirmation before continuing to promote the published account details or QR; verify account name, payee identity, IFSC/account details, processor configuration, descriptor, currency/eligibility, and no hidden recurring option. Maintain a dated internal verification log and publish only a non-sensitive “last verified” date once verified.

### Privacy and forms

Add a donor-specific Privacy section stating: collection fields, purpose, processor/handler, retention period or criteria, security/access controls, payment credential handling, request/deletion route subject to legal record requirements, and an absolute no-publication rule for donor name, amount, or payment reference absent affirmative opt-in and a lawful basis. There should still be **no donor wall**, even with opt-in, under this plan.

Place a concise nearby privacy notice/link beside contact, volunteer, donation, and referral forms. Volunteer/referral routes need enforceable safeguarding boundaries: no unsupervised child contact, no direct/private child messaging, no informal collection of child data, no identifying/geotagged media, authorised/supervised activity only, consent-required media, escalation contact, and removal/review process. Do not publish a policy that cannot be enforced operationally.

### Financial transparency and bilingual parity

Add an anchored **Financial transparency and reports** section on Board and Transparency. It must show the report period, document type, source, status, and publication/review date for every item. If no verified public report is ready, say so plainly and offer an information-request route; do not claim annual reports, audits, filings, staff capacity, “every rupee” tracking, CSR eligibility, or scheduled publication without evidence. All statutory status (CIN, Section 8, NGO DARPAN, 12AB/80G, foreign-contribution status, audit/filing obligations) needs independent factual/legal confirmation before publication.

Control English and Odia legal/payment copy as paired content. Review both versions every time donation, acknowledgement, refund, 80G/12AB, privacy, safeguarding, financial disclosures, or payment details change. Add visible effective date and revision date to Privacy, Terms, and Donation and Refund Policy.

### Required go/no-go checklist for each record

Publish only when all answers are **yes**:

- Is every asserted fact traceable to a primary or otherwise verified source?
- Is the date precise and reconciled, or deliberately generalised?
- Is the aggregate count bounded to the event and accompanied by a limitation where needed?
- Does the card say historical/non-flagship and avoid a current-programme inference?
- Have private child/family/founder details been removed or withheld?
- Is media omitted or documented as consent-reviewed, safeguarding-approved, rights-cleared, and technically controlled?
- Is any press reference attributed and permitted for the chosen public use?
- Is the English/Odia meaning equivalent?
- Does the canonical/redirect target use `www` and lead to a useful, non-misleading page?
- Has the designated owner approved publication and next review date?

**Any “no” means no publication, or a narrower text-only version.**

## 10. Final restoration decision

Restore **records, not legacy experiences**. The public site should offer a defensible, quiet evidence trail: two reviewed historical education records within an education-accountability context and three reviewed, limited non-flagship support records in a secondary archive. It should not revive the prior blog/gallery/media architecture, founder-centred storytelling, broad programme claims, donor exposure, old financial assertions, or recurring-payment mechanics.

This approach preserves useful public provenance while protecting children, elders, founders, donors, and the Foundation from inaccurate claims, privacy harm, copyright risk, and user confusion. It also makes the current direction unmistakable: **education first; limited verified support secondary; Vidyapitha/elder care planned, not active.**
