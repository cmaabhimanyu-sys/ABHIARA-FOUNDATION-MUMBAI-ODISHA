# Abhiara Foundation Owner Guide

## Your one website control centre

Open:

**https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/admin**

Sign in with **abhiarafoundation@gmail.com**. The confirmed owner account **cma.abhimanyu@gmail.com** also has administrator access and may be used if needed. Identity verification briefly opens on the supported secure login host and then returns you to the official `.org` owner control centre. Photo uploads and other routine updates are completed on the official website. A small **Owner Login** link is also available in the website footer.

If an old sign in tab shows an expired or invalid relay message, close that tab, open the admin address above again and press **Continue to secure sign in**. Do not refresh the old relay address.

You do not need to open GitHub, edit code, or work inside Vercel for routine website updates.

## Start with Press and Media

Open **Press and Media** in the left menu. It has four tools for updating public information:

1. **Add report content** for a verified activity record.
2. **Upload and publish impact photos** through Photo Library.
3. **Edit captions or remove a photo from public view** through Impact Photos.
4. **Add a public video** by pasting a reviewed YouTube link.
5. **Update social media links** for Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn and Instagram.

For a completed RTI or human rights class, choose **Community archive** when adding the report. Enter the checked date, broad place, short subject and total attendance only. Do not enter participant names, certificate numbers or unredacted certificate copies.

The public result appears at:

**https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/rti-human-rights-awareness**

and in the checked records on:

**https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/press-and-media**

Publish information and media only after you have checked the facts, permission and privacy points below.

## What you can manage yourself

### 1. Monthly Reports

Use this to publish verified activity content.

Enter:

- A short public title
- What support was provided
- The activity date or report month
- A district or broad location
- An aggregate result, such as `50+ children`
- One approved photo, if suitable

Published education reports appear on:

**https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/monthly-reports**

They can also appear in the Press and Media public record. Records in other categories appear in the verified-support archive. They are not shown as regular programmes.

### 2. Photo Library

Upload photographs only after consent has been checked. They go to the Vercel Blob folder:

`abhiara-images/`

Choose the right photo type:

- **Beneficiary photos** for the protected Student Impact workflow
- **Programme photos** for reviewed programme media
- **Press and media proofs** for photographs that may appear on the Press and Media page
- **Elder support ground work** for reviewed visits and limited elder help
- **Disaster relief ground work** for reviewed local relief activity
- **Medical emergency ground work** only when public permission and privacy checks are complete
- **Animal welfare ground work** for reviewed feeding, treatment help or rescue coordination
- **Leadership portraits**, **Partner logos** or **General website photos** for their stated uses

Rules:

- JPEG, PNG or WebP only
- Keep each image below 1 MB
- Add a clear public description
- Confirm that permission for public use has been recorded for beneficiary, programme, press, elder, disaster, medical and animal welfare photos
- Never upload Aadhaar, bank passbooks, school records, home addresses, personal phone numbers, vehicle numbers or payment details
- Keep names and private family circumstances out of filenames and descriptions

For every impact photo:

1. Choose the right photo type.
2. Select the image file.
3. Write a short public title.
4. Write a factual public description without private personal details.
5. Choose the **Website category**. The category decides which public section will show the photo.
6. Add the activity date and a district or broad location where known.
7. Confirm permission and privacy review.
8. Tick **Publish after upload** if it is ready for the website. Leave it unticked to save a draft.
9. Press **Upload and Publish** or **Upload as Draft**.

A published photo may appear in the homepage rolling photo section, Impact Gallery, Press and Media, and its matching cause page. A draft stays private.

### 3. Impact Photos

Use **Impact Photos** to manage every uploaded public photo.

- **Edit** changes the title, description, category, location, date and public visibility.
- **First on homepage** makes one published photo the fixed first slide at the top of the homepage.
- **Earlier** and **Later** change the order of the remaining rolling photos.
- **Display order** can be entered directly when a specific position is needed.
- **Publish** makes a reviewed draft live.
- **Unpublish** removes the photo from public pages without deleting its Blob file.
- **Delete record** removes the website entry but keeps the stored file in Photo Library.
- To permanently delete the stored file, open **Photo Library**, choose its folder and press **Delete**. A warning appears before deletion.

The homepage and public galleries read only published records. Uploading a file alone never makes it public.

### 4. Public Videos

Paste a reviewed YouTube link instead of uploading a large video file.

Before adding it:

- Watch the complete video
- Check the title, thumbnail, speech and background signs
- Confirm permission for any recognisable person
- Do not publish private child details, school records, phone numbers, addresses, bank details or family hardship information
- Use the Education or Documentary category for a video that may appear in Press and Media

### 5. Social Links

Add or replace the official HTTPS links for:

- Facebook
- YouTube
- LinkedIn
- Instagram

The website checks that each link uses the matching social platform. These links appear in the footer, Contact page and Press and Media page.

### 6. Public Details

Use this to update only reviewed public values:

- Children actively supported
- Verified activities
- Families supported in verified cases
- Districts represented in public records
- Public email address
- Public WhatsApp channel link

Only values used by an approved public page are displayed. Legal details, payment settings and protected records remain locked.

### 7. Board, Members and Advisors

Use **Board, Members and Advisors** to manage every approved Abhiara person shown on the public people page.

- Choose **Board of Directors** only for a director named in the Foundation's official company records.
- Choose **Members** for people who hold an approved Foundation role but are not directors or advisors.
- Choose **Advisory Members** for people who guide the Foundation but are not directors.
- Add the English and Odia name, role, qualification and short biography.
- Add an approved public profile photo and an HTTPS public profile link where available.
- Use **Display order** to control the order on the website.
- Use **Publish** or **Unpublish** to show or hide a profile without deleting it.
- Use **Delete** only when the profile should be removed from the owner records.

Published profiles appear at:

**https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/board-and-transparency**

### 8. Successful Donations

This is a read-only payment record.

It shows only completed payments that have a verified payment ID. There is no button to create a donation manually, so test or fake donation records cannot be added through the dashboard.

### 9. Volunteer Applications and Contact Messages

Review genuine submissions received through the website forms. Do not copy personal details into public reports.

## Safe publishing routine

Before publishing any report, photo or video:

1. Confirm the activity actually happened.
2. Confirm the date, broad location and aggregate result.
3. Confirm permission for public use.
4. Remove or hide identity cards, bank documents, phone numbers, vehicle numbers, exact addresses and school records.
5. Avoid names and personal hardship details unless there is a clear approved public reason.
6. Use simple factual language.
7. Publish only after review.

## Changes that remain protected

Ask your authorised technical maintainer to handle these changes because they affect legal accuracy, payment safety or the site structure:

- Company filings and official director appointments or resignations
- CIN, NGO Darpan and tax-status wording
- Bank details and payment QR
- Payment keys, database credentials and Vercel secrets
- Main navigation and website design
- New programme categories
- Deleting payment or donor records
- Changing the official domain

## If something does not work

- **Cannot sign in:** confirm you are using the Foundation owner account.
- **Photo storage unavailable:** the website administrator should check the Vercel Blob connection and production environment settings.
- **A public change does not appear:** reload the page once. Admin-published content normally appears without a code deployment.
- **A structural website change is needed:** update the authorised GitHub repository, test the change and deploy it through Vercel.

## Important distinction

Routine content changes made in the owner dashboard update the database or Blob storage directly. They do not require a GitHub commit.

Changes to design, legal wording, payments, security, routes or software features require a reviewed GitHub change and Vercel deployment.
