# Abhiara Foundation Owner Guide

## Your one website control centre

Open:

**https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/admin**

Sign in with **abhiarafoundation@gmail.com**. The confirmed owner account **cma.abhimanyu@gmail.com** also has administrator access and may be used if needed. Identity verification briefly opens on the supported secure login host and then returns you to the official `.org` owner control centre. Photo uploads and other routine updates are completed on the official website. A small **Owner Login** link is also available in the website footer.

If an old sign in tab shows an expired or invalid relay message, close that tab, open the admin address above again and press **Continue to secure sign in**. Do not refresh the old relay address.

You do not need to open GitHub, edit code, or work inside Vercel for routine website updates.

## Start with Press and Media

Open **Press and Media** in the left menu. It has five tools for updating public information:

1. **Add report content** for a verified activity record.
2. **Upload and publish impact photos** through Photo Library.
3. **Edit captions or remove a photo from public view** through Impact Photos.
4. **Add a public video** by pasting a reviewed YouTube link.
5. **Update social media links** for Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn and Instagram.

For a completed RTI or human rights class, choose **Community archive** when adding the report. Enter the checked date, broad place, short subject and total attendance only. Do not enter participant names, certificate numbers or unredacted certificate copies.

The public result appears at:

**https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/rti-human-rights-awareness**

and in the checked records on:

**https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/press-and-media**

Publish information and media only after you have checked the facts, permission and privacy points below.

## What you can manage yourself

### 1. Monthly Reports

Use this to publish verified activity content.

Enter:

- A short public title
- What support was provided
- The activity date or report month
- A district or broad location
- An aggregate result only when its date, definition and source programme record have been checked
- One approved photo, if suitable

Published education reports appear on:

**https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/monthly-reports**

They can also appear in the Press and Media public record. Records in other categories appear in the verified-support archive. They are not shown as regular programmes.

**Programme updates and photos are not financial statements.** The public Monthly Reports page has separate financial, programme and photo sections. No reconciled financial amounts are published there yet. A photograph review does not hold up a separate anonymised financial summary, but a financial summary needs source records, reconciliation and a final review. Never put donor names, child identifiers, bank accounts, payment IDs or private bills into a public report. Do not describe an internal summary as audited; an audited annual report is a different document. Publishing monthly financial amounts is **not yet an Admin feature**. Ask the authorised maintainer to add a controlled publishing workflow after the books and disclosure format have been agreed.

### 2. Photo Library

Upload photographs only after consent has been checked. They go to the Vercel Blob folder:

`abhiara-images/`

Choose the right photo type:

- **Beneficiary photos** for the protected Student Impact workflow
- **Programme photos** for reviewed programme media
- **Press and media proofs** for photographs that may appear on the Press and Media page
- **Elder support ground work** for reviewed visits and limited elder help
- **Disaster relief ground work** for reviewed local relief activity
- **Medical emergency ground work** only when public permission and privacy checks are complete
- **Animal welfare ground work** for reviewed feeding, treatment help or rescue coordination
- **Leadership portraits**, **Partner logos** or **General website photos** for their stated uses

Rules:

- JPEG, PNG or WebP only
- Keep each image below 1 MB
- Add a clear public description
- Confirm that permission for public use has been recorded for beneficiary, programme, press, elder, disaster, medical and animal welfare photos
- Never upload Aadhaar, bank passbooks, school records, home addresses, personal phone numbers, vehicle numbers or payment details
- Keep names and private family circumstances out of filenames and descriptions
- For a recognisable elderly person, record consent for the specific public photo, video or story. Ask the person directly wherever they can decide for themselves. Family, caregiver or institution permission does not replace the person’s own consent.
- Do not publish an elderly person’s identity documents, bank or pension details, exact address, phone number, diagnosis, prescription, disability details, or private family and financial circumstances.
- If an elderly person cannot provide informed consent, keep the content private unless an authorised representative’s permission and the Foundation’s safeguarding approval are both recorded.
- Remove or unpublish elderly media from future public use if consent is withdrawn.

For every impact photo:

1. Choose the right photo type.
2. Select the image file.
3. Write a short public title.
4. Write a factual public description without private personal details.
5. Choose the **Website category**. The category decides which public section will show the photo.
6. Add the activity date and a district or broad location where known.
7. Confirm permission and privacy review.
8. Tick **Publish after upload** if it is ready for the website. Leave it unticked to save a draft.
9. Press **Upload and Publish** or **Upload as Draft**.

A published photo may appear in the homepage rolling photo section, Impact Gallery, Press and Media, and its matching cause page. A draft stays private.

**Upload a programme album:** Open **Photo Library → Upload a programme album**. Choose the destination, select 1–20 distinct JPEG/PNG/WebP files (each below 1 MB), and enter one bilingual programme title, activity caption, month/date and broad location using `English | ଓଡ଼ିଆ`. Confirm guardian/adult media permissions and safeguarding review for every selected photo. Tick **Publish this album** and press **Upload and publish album**, or leave it unticked for drafts. The files go to Vercel Blob and create individual, editable **Impact Photos** records; activity/beneficiary totals are not created or multiplied. Uploads run sequentially. On an error, keep this page open and press **Resume unfinished photos**; saved files are reused and existing caption records are reconciled. Do not reload or leave the page mid-upload—the retry state is only retained in this open page. Programme details lock once a photo has been stored, so retries cannot mix albums. After completion, press **Start another album**. If you closed an interrupted page, check Photo Library and Impact Photos before reselecting files to avoid duplicate uploads. Blob URLs are public even for draft records, so never upload unreviewed private media as a draft.

**One programme, one photo group:** Impact Gallery and the separate Disaster, Medical and Animal pages group photographs only when their **category, programme title, date/month and broad location all match**. Each full-width group has one heading, one description and **up to five full-frame photos**, followed by **View all photos**. If only two approved photos exist, it shows two—not invented or repeated fillers. The two known Pratibha Samman team/programme views group together only when their date, place and cause match. The viewer retains every unique published photograph, full-frame, with next/previous buttons, keyboard arrows and thumbnails. Missing titles, dates or places are not guessed; those records remain separate. Use the same bilingual event title/date/place for photos that belong together, and distinct metadata for different events. Under **Impact Photos**, use **Earlier / Later / Display order** to place the best four or five different views first. The owner-selected **First on homepage** photo remains the first Home slide. No stored photo, CMS record or consent status is deleted or unpublished by this layout change. For the Raisar books-and-dictionary event, all 18 published photos remain available within one album; Mustafapur retains its separate ten-photo album. Album/photo totals are not activity or beneficiary totals.

### 3. Impact Photos

Use **Impact Photos** to manage every uploaded public photo.

- **Edit** changes the title, description, category, location, date and public visibility.
- **First on homepage** makes one published photo the fixed first slide at the top of the homepage.
- **Earlier** and **Later** change the order of the remaining rolling photos.
- **Display order** can be entered directly when a specific position is needed.
- **Publish** makes a reviewed draft live.
- **Unpublish** removes the photo from public pages without deleting its Blob file.
- **Delete record** removes the website entry but keeps the stored file in Photo Library.
- To permanently delete the stored file, open **Photo Library**, choose its folder and press **Delete**. A warning appears before deletion.

The homepage and public galleries read only published records. Uploading a file alone never makes it public.

**Review photos already online:** The published-photo flag records website visibility, not the location of a signed consent form. Review the underlying permission record for recognisable children and older people, including photos that predate the current Admin workflow. If a record cannot be found or consent for public use has been withdrawn, use **Unpublish** until permission and safeguarding review are resolved. Keep signed permissions and beneficiary details in a restricted owner file, never in public image descriptions or the website repository.

### 4. Public Videos

Open **Admin → Public Videos → Add Video**. Paste a reviewed YouTube link **or** acknowledge the recorded permissions and use **Choose a reviewed MP4 video** to upload a compressed file under 2.4 MB to `abhiara-images/programme-videos/` in Vercel Blob. Once the URL appears, enter an English/Odia title and description separated by `|`, select **Education**, and press **Add Video**. Uploading the file alone does not publish the CMS entry; the Blob URL itself is public, so do not upload drafts without permission. The video plays only after a visitor clicks Play on **Press and Media**. The top media strip and Student Impact link appear when a reviewed Education or Documentary video is published. Public media never autoplay.

Before adding it:

- Watch the complete video
- Check the title, thumbnail, speech, background signs, music rights and any on-screen payment QR
- Confirm recorded permission for every recognisable person, including guardian permission for children, and permission to display any other organisation's logo
- Do not publish private child or elderly details, school or health records, phone numbers, addresses, bank or pension details, identity documents or family hardship information
- For the October 2026 story footage, use only the **corrected longer cut**; do not upload the original longer source with its inaccurate all-orphaned statement
- Use **Edit** to revise title or caption. **Delete** removes both an Abhiara-hosted MP4 file and its public record; a YouTube link only removes its record. Keep signed consent documents outside the website repository. If consent is withdrawn, delete the affected hosted video as soon as possible, including its Blob object.

### 8. Successful Donations

This is a read-only payment record.

It shows only completed payments that have a verified payment ID. There is no button to create a donation manually, so test or fake donation records cannot be added through the dashboard.

For refund requests, use the published **Donation and Refund Policy**. Check the payment reference against the actual payment record and reply from the official Foundation email. The published target is to acknowledge within **3 working days** and communicate a decision within **10 working days after sufficient verification information is received**. Review later requests too, especially duplicates and unauthorised payments. A verified duplicate or Foundation error is refundable. Ordinarily refund to the original payment method; do not deduct payment charges for a verified duplicate or Foundation error. Never request a card PIN, password or one-time password. A Donation Acknowledgement is not an 80G certificate. Admin's Successful Donations view is read-only and does not issue refunds.

## Safe publishing routine

Before publishing any report, photo or video:

1. Confirm the activity actually happened.
2. Confirm the date, broad location and aggregate result.
3. Confirm permission for public use.
4. Remove or hide identity cards, bank documents, phone numbers, vehicle numbers, exact addresses and school records.
5. Avoid names and personal hardship details unless there is a clear approved public reason.
6. Use simple factual language.
7. Publish only after review.

## Changes that remain protected

Ask your authorised technical maintainer to handle these changes because they affect legal accuracy, payment safety or the site structure:

- Company filings and official director appointments or resignations
- CIN, NGO Darpan and tax-status wording
- Bank details and payment QR

## If something does not work

- **Cannot sign in:** confirm you are using the Foundation owner account.
- **Photo storage unavailable:** the website administrator should check the Vercel Blob connection and production environment settings.
- **A public change does not appear:** reload the page once. Admin-published content normally appears without a code deployment.
- **A structural website change is needed:** update the authorised GitHub repository, test the change and deploy it through Vercel.

## Important distinction

Routine content changes made in the owner dashboard update the database or Blob storage directly. They do not require a GitHub commit.

Changes to design, legal wording, payments, security, routes or software features require a reviewed GitHub change and Vercel deployment.
