## Patterns worth using

- Keep the header short and move detail into clear dropdowns and the footer.
- Lead with one precise statement of purpose, then show current work, dated updates, photos, reports and legal information.
- Keep media, field updates and formal reports separate.
- Use dated captions, safe place information and modest facts instead of large unsupported counters.
- Give the owner a structured publishing workflow rather than making every uploaded file public.
- Show only photos that pass permission and privacy checks.
- Give public photos meaningful titles, captions, cause labels and dates where known.
- Keep an easy Unpublish control so an image can be removed quickly.

## What Abhiara should not copy

- Large national reach claims, partnership claims, awards or government alignment without evidence.
- Identifiable beneficiary stories, medical details, addresses, identity papers or private family histories.
- A crowded mega menu or a large newsroom that cannot be maintained.
- Recurring giving, foreign contribution routes, tax deduction claims or restricted-fund promises that are not operational and verified.
- Photos that go live simply because they were uploaded.

## Recommended Abhiara homepage order

1. Education-first hero and one clear action.
2. Clear work-area cards with honest current, past or planned status.
3. Owner-published rolling field photos with title, caption and cause.
4. Founder story, future vision, Press and Media, and Impact Gallery links.
5. Limited verified assistance with clear capacity and privacy limits.
6. One-time donation option with accurate legal and tax wording.
7. Transparency, reports, contact and policies.

## Recommended photo publishing workflow

1. Owner signs in at `https://www.abhiarafoundation.org/admin`.
2. Owner opens **Photo Library** and chooses the correct cause folder.
3. Owner uploads a file below 1 MB and confirms permission and privacy review.
4. Owner adds a public title and short factual caption.
5. Owner either saves it as a draft or checks **Publish after upload**.
6. A published photo appears in the homepage carousel, Impact Gallery and the matching public cause page.
7. The **Impact Photos** tab lets the owner edit title, caption, cause and public visibility.
8. **Unpublish** removes it from public pages without deleting the Blob file.

This structure uses the strongest transferable patterns from the reviewed organisations without copying their scale, beneficiary-identification practices or operational claims.
