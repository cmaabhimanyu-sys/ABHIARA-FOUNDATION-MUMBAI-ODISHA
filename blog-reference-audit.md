# Blog Reference Audit

## Pratham patterns worth adapting

Pratham separates real human stories from broader media and publications. Its homepage uses a short Stories of Change introduction, compact story cards with a name and excerpt, and a separate Media and Publications list. Resources are discoverable from the main navigation without making every item a top-level link.

The Stories of Change archive uses a strong documentary hero, a simple three-column story grid, short human excerpts, Read More links, numbered pagination, and a separate featured-video area. The Blogs archive uses the same clear hierarchy but carries reflections, programme explanations, field notes, and organisational updates rather than beneficiary profiles. This separation is useful for Abhiara because it avoids presenting every activity as a personal success story.

For Abhiara, the useful pattern is a clear archive with a featured story, category filters, a short excerpt, date, location, and a dedicated article view. The visual treatment should continue Abhiara's current amber, charcoal, cream, serif-heading, and documentary-photo system rather than copying Pratham's brand.

## Adaptation limits

The suggested topics in the supplied image are editorial ideas, not verified Abhiara activities. The first release must not claim digital learning centres, scholarships, health camps, CSR successes, or beneficiary transformation stories unless the Foundation later supplies evidence and consent.

Abhiara currently has two published CMS posts, but both contain outdated wording and CDN image URLs. The new public blog should use corrected verified records and registered `/images/` assets. Activities should remain the factual event log. Monthly Impact should remain the month-by-month evidence archive. The blog should explain selected verified activities in a readable story format without repeating the full galleries.

## Proposed public structure

Use `/blog` with the title **Stories, Insights and Impact | Abhiara Foundation**. Initial public categories should be limited to **Education Support**, **Jeevan Sathi**, **Emergency Relief**, **Events and Activities**, and **Foundation Updates**. Planned categories such as Community Development, Youth and Sports, Environment and Animals, and Culture and Heritage should not appear until verified work exists.

Each article card should include one registered image, category, title, two or three lines of excerpt, verified date and location, and a Read More link. Article pages should include the full story, evidence note, related Monthly Impact link, and a route back to the archive.

The first Abhiara release should use one combined archive because the Foundation does not yet have enough consented beneficiary stories for two separate archives. The page can visually distinguish **Field Stories** from **Foundation Updates** while Activities remains the event log and Monthly Impact remains the evidence record.
