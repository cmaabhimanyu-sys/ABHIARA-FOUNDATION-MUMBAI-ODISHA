# Abhiara Foundation Navigation Benchmark Decision

Use **four editorial groups**, one English and Odia control, and one separate **Donate once** button. Home stays on the logo. This is the final header structure:

**Logo | About Abhiara | Education work | Impact and transparency | Get involved | English / ଓଡ଼ିଆ | Donate once**

This approach follows a consistent pattern across UNICEF, charity: water, Pratham, Save the Children, BRAC, and the Gates Foundation. Their public websites separate the donation action from the main information structure and keep governance, evidence, and contact routes discoverable. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

Abhiara should follow that principle without copying the scale or complexity of those organisations. The Foundation has one active education programme, limited verified support, a small historical record, and three future plans. Its navigation should make those differences clear.

## The four groups

### About Abhiara

The parent link opens **Our Story**. Its small menu contains:

- Our Story
- Board and Transparency
- Privacy and Child Safeguarding
- Contact

This keeps purpose, people, legal information, safeguarding, and the public contact route close together. Leading organisations make these trust routes easy to find, although their governance systems are much larger than Abhiara needs. [7] [8] [9] [10] [11] [12]

### Education work

The parent link opens **Abhiara Shiksha Sathi**, the Foundation’s one active flagship programme. Its menu contains:

- Abhiara Shiksha Sathi
- How We Support a Child
- Limited Verified Support
- Vision and Upcoming

The future school, elder care home, and livelihood centre remain together on **Vision and Upcoming** and must stay clearly labelled as planned work, not current programmes.

### Impact and transparency

The parent link opens **Student Impact**. Its menu contains:

- Student Impact
- Monthly Reports
- Press and Media
- Transparency and Documents

This name is clearer than “Records.” It describes public evidence and accountability without suggesting that private child or family records are available.

### Get involved

The parent link opens **Volunteer**. Its menu contains:

- Donate once for Education
- Partners and Supporters
- Volunteer
- Birthday with Purpose

The main Donate once button remains outside this menu so giving is always clear and visible.

## Desktop behaviour

At 1280 px and 1440 px, the header should remain one calm line. Every group name is a real page link. A separate chevron opens a small text-only menu with no more than five links. The menus work by click, touch, and keyboard rather than hover alone.

Do not add search, social icons, an account button, a country selector, promotional tiles, metrics, or photographs to the header. The full website directory belongs in the footer.

## Mobile behaviour

At 390 px, retain the logo, language control, Donate button, and labelled Menu button. The opened panel uses the same four groups as desktop. Each group has a direct parent link and a separate expand button. Only one level opens, so the visitor never enters a drill-down maze.

The mobile panel repeats Donate once at the top. Privacy, safeguarding, donation policy, terms, and contact remain visible at the bottom.

## Accessibility requirements

The logo has an accessible Home name. The active route uses `aria-current="page"`. Menu buttons expose their expanded state and controlled panel. The mobile menu behaves as a dialog, moves focus inside, closes with Escape or a visible Close button, restores focus after closing, and prevents background scrolling while open.

All important controls use at least a 44 by 44 CSS-pixel target. The same information structure is maintained in English and Odia. Navigation never publishes child names, schools, precise locations, family circumstances, or case histories.

## Patterns Abhiara should not copy

Do not copy global-scale mega menus, deep cascading sections, country networks, large newsrooms, donor accounts, public fundraising pages, recurring giving, sponsorship products, child-linked donation journeys, unsupported counters, or large report archives. Those systems reflect the staff, data, and fundraising models of mature international organisations. [1] [2] [4] [5] [6]

Abhiara’s stronger choice is a smaller structure that the owner can keep accurate: one education programme, a clear evidence path, visible governance and safeguarding, honest future plans, and one-time giving.
