# Abhiara Blog Content Plan

## Public title

**Stories, Insights and Impact | Abhiara Foundation**

The page will be available at `/blog`. It will use English and Odia through the existing language switch.

## Categories for the first release

| Category | What belongs here | First-release status |
|---|---|---|
| Education Support | Verified school events and ongoing student support | Active |
| Jeevan Sathi | Verified visits and practical family or elder support | Active |
| Emergency Relief | Verified fire, flood, cyclone, medical, or death-case support | Active |
| Events and Activities | Verified community service events | Active |
| Foundation Updates | Registration, governance, public information, and official notices | No article until a useful verified update is ready |

Planned programmes such as Health and Wellness, Community Development, Youth and Sports, Environment and Animals, and Culture and Heritage will not appear as blog categories until real work is documented.

## Writing rules

Each article will use simple factual paragraphs. It will state what happened, when, where, and what the public record shows. It will not invent quotations, names, personal histories, diagnoses, family details, outcomes, or emotional transformation. It will not promise a future programme. Each article will link to Monthly Impact for supporting photos and videos.

The two old CMS articles will not be used as fallback public copy because they include unverified statements about tribal children, walking distances, loneliness, legal needs, health check-ups, permanent learning centres, and future volunteer models. The existing CMS editor can remain for future verified posts, but the public page will use the reviewed bilingual article data until the CMS supports bilingual fields and evidence links.
