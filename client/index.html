Our focus

Education is our main work. Through Abhiara Shiksha Sathi, we help orphaned children and children from underprivileged families stay in school across India.

Abhiara Shiksha Sathi

Verified, need-based education support may include monthly tuition fees, school bags, books, stationery, uniforms, examination needs and learning materials. More than 50 children are actively supported.

How support works

  1. Referral or request
  2. Ground verification
  3. Need and capacity review
  4. Documented approval
  5. Traceable payment
  6. Follow-up

Limited verified support

Alongside education, Abhiara Foundation provides limited, verified support for vulnerable elders, animal welfare, medical emergencies and disaster relief, subject to available funds, ground verification and approved budget.
