# Abhiara Foundation: Global Programme Benchmark 2026

## Recommended public programme structure

**Keep one active programme: Education.** Abhiara should be presented as an independent, local, education-first foundation—not as a miniature version of a global multi-sector NGO. UNICEF India itself concentrates its education programme around early childhood education, out-of-school children, and quality teaching and learning; that is a useful example of focus, not a claim of any relationship or shared reach. [1] Abhiara is **not affiliated with or endorsed by UNICEF** unless it can publish a formal, verifiable relationship.

Use these six clearly labelled website categories. They answer the requested homepage needs without turning global organisations’ portfolios into a checklist.

1. **Education — Active now.** This is the only active programme category. It should show only documented local learning work already delivered: the learner group, place, dates, activity, Abhiara’s role, partner role, and factual outputs. Suitable labels include **learning support**, **reading activities**, **mentoring**, **learning materials**, **school/community sessions**, or **facilitator support**, but only where records support them. Do not claim learning gains, school retention, inclusion, wellbeing, or reach unless Abhiara has measured and can explain them.
2. **Community Help — Limited case-based help.** This is not a service portfolio. It may describe a small, discretionary, referral-led route for individual requests concerning older people, medical emergencies, disaster-affected households, or animals. State the limit plainly: **“We consider a small number of local requests case by case. We are not an emergency, medical, disaster-response, elder-care or animal-rescue provider.”**
3. **Past Learning Activities — Past verified work.** Place former RTI and human-rights classes here, with dates, venue/locality, topic, facilitator or partner, and documented attendance. Call them **past awareness and learning activities**, not a current legal-service or human-rights programme.
4. **Digital Learning and AI Basics — Future plan.** Label this **“In development”** or **“Future plan—not currently delivered.”** Do not collect registrations, promise a course, issue certificates, or present an app or AI tool until the readiness conditions below are met.
5. **Wellness and Wellbeing — Future plan.** This may describe a possible partner-led health-awareness and basic check-up model. It must remain clearly marked as not active until qualified providers, written roles, consent, privacy controls, referral steps, records and approved funding are ready. It is not a clinic, treatment service or medical-emergency route.
6. **Evidence, Updates and Governance.** Publish dated activity updates, the difference between outputs and outcomes, safeguarding and privacy information that is actually in use, partner roles, and a contact/referral route. This follows the useful transparency principle of documenting planning, delivery, follow-up and partner roles, scaled to Abhiara’s actual capacity. [2]

This narrow structure is credible because large organisations have purpose-built specialist portfolios and operating systems. Save the Children publicly groups its work across health, education, protection, resilience and emergencies; BRAC spans education, health, finance, livelihoods, humanitarian response and enterprises. [3] [4] Their scope is not a menu for a small education-first foundation.

## Homepage sections and plain-English copy

### 1. Hero: the public promise

> **Local education support, delivered with care and evidence.**  
> Abhiara Foundation focuses on verified education work in [locality]. We work with learners, families, schools and local partners where roles are clear.

Replace **[locality]** only with the actual area served. Do not say “national,” “state-wide,” “for the most vulnerable,” or “every child” unless records establish that claim.

### 2. Our active focus: Education

Use a single featured block:

> **Education — Active now**  
> We share our completed education activities with the date, place, learner group, what happened and the evidence available. We report delivery outputs separately from learning outcomes.

Below it, show only real activity cards. Each card should contain: **what happened; where; period; intended group; actual attendance/reach; Abhiara’s role; partner/facilitator; materials or session record; and a short feedback or observation note.** This borrows the clarity of Pratham’s programme pages—learner group, method, materials and measurement—without borrowing Pratham’s scale, named models, government partnerships, research, or claimed results. [5]

### 3. How we work

Use four short steps: **Listen locally → agree roles with the school/community or partner → deliver safely → record and report.** Publish only partnerships that are real, permitted, and accurately described. Keep human facilitation central. Accessible materials, local-language support, caregiver feedback and facilitator training can be introduced in phases when they are actually available.

This is more suitable than copying a global education portfolio. UNICEF’s education work includes inclusive education, emergencies, early childhood, primary education, adolescent skills, digital learning and global partnerships; its scale and mandate are not transferable. [6]

### 4. Community Help: limited case-based support

Use one restrained block, not four programme cards:

> **Limited case-based help**  
> Where resources, checks and a safe referral route are available, we may consider a small number of local requests. Decisions are case by case, subject to available funds and partner capacity. We do not provide medical treatment, emergency response, residential care, legal advice, disaster logistics or animal sheltering.

Within that page, organise requests as **Older people**, **Medical emergencies**, **Disaster-affected households**, and **Animals**. For each, publish the same boundaries:

- **Older people:** limited practical signposting or referral and, only if formally approved, a bounded one-off support decision. Do not claim healthy-ageing, income-security, rights protection, elder-abuse response, home care, residential care or pension delivery. HelpAge’s thematic work includes humanitarian action, health, income security, rights and ageism; it illustrates why such work needs distinct capacity rather than a generic “elder help” label. [7]
- **Medical emergencies:** referral or carefully documented, exceptional financial assistance only where legal, safely assessed and funded. Do **not** assess conditions, give medical advice, operate transport, procure treatment, handle patient data beyond necessity, or claim medical outcomes. It is not a health programme.
- **Natural-disaster relief:** share verified public/partner referral contacts and make only bounded, partner-led support where a qualified relief organisation is responsible for assessment and delivery. Do **not** call this disaster relief, emergency response, resilience, distribution, shelter, rescue, WASH or humanitarian logistics unless Abhiara has that authorised operating capability.
- **Animal welfare:** signpost to verified, appropriate local veterinary or rescue services and consider only documented, lawful, limited assistance. Do **not** claim rescue, treatment, shelter, adoption, sterilisation, wildlife care, or an animal-welfare programme without qualified providers, permissions, funding and records.

A request is not automatically an entitlement. Before any case is shown publicly, use a written case protocol: eligibility and maximum support; conflict-of-interest check; responsible decision-maker; safeguarding/privacy assessment; partner/referral verification; receipt and closure record; and consent. Never publish a child’s or patient’s sensitive information, image, story, school name or exact location without valid consent and a usable safeguarding/data-protection process.

### 5. Past learning activities: RTI and human-rights classes

Show this as an archive, with the label **“Past verified work — not an active legal-service programme.”** Recommended copy:

> **Past awareness and learning classes**  
> Abhiara previously held RTI and human-rights learning sessions in [place] during [period]. This page records the topic, facilitator, attendance evidence and any partner role. It does not offer legal advice, RTI filing, case representation, certificate verification or a current human-rights service.

If certificates were issued, say only **“certificates were issued to recorded participants”** when Abhiara holds the underlying attendance, issuer and issue-date records. Do not call certificates accredited, government-recognised, verified, or professionally qualifying without independent evidence and authority to make that statement. Provide a private records contact instead of public verification badges or participant lists.

### 6. Future plan: Digital Learning and AI Basics

Use a future-only card:

> **Digital Learning and AI Basics — Future plan**  
> We are assessing whether a small, facilitated local learning pilot would be useful and accessible. It is not yet a programme, app, course or certificate offer.

A credible first step is a short discovery and design phase: assess learner age, language, device access, connectivity, accessibility needs, facilitator availability and a real learning problem; consult learners, caregivers and schools; choose low-tech or blended options; prepare child-safety, privacy and consent controls; train facilitators; then decide whether a small, time-bound pilot is justified. Pratham’s digital work is integrated with a wider education model and has dedicated technology, content and learning infrastructure; its example supports assessing access and using technology in support of learning, not copying tools or deploying AI by default. [8]

If a pilot is eventually approved, it should have a defined cohort, human facilitator, accessible/offline option where needed, attendance records, a simple baseline and follow-up check, learner/caregiver feedback, and published limitations. Do not claim AI literacy, online-safety outcomes, learning improvement, employability, certification or scale before there is credible evidence.

## What belongs in each status now

| Status | Website content | Conditions and wording |
|---|---|---|
| **Active now** | **Education only** | Use only dated, local, verifiable delivery. State outputs such as sessions, attendance and materials; label outcomes only when measured. |
| **Limited case-based help** | Older people; medical emergency requests; disaster-affected households; animals | A request/referral route, not four programmes. Case-by-case, resource-limited, privacy-protected and referral-led. Never promise treatment, rescue, care, legal help or relief operations. |
| **Past verified work** | RTI and human-rights classes | Archive facts and evidence. No current legal advice, advocacy, RTI filing, certificate-verification or outcome claims. |
| **Future plan** | Digital Learning and AI Basics; Wellness and Wellbeing | Clearly marked not active. Begin only after access, safeguarding, qualified partners, written roles, consent, referral, funding and measurement readiness are documented for the relevant plan. |
| **Do not add yet** | See below | Do not create a navigation item, donation appeal or delivery claim for these areas. |

## Do not add yet

Do not add **healthcare, clinics, medical camps, ambulances, medical fundraising promises, mental-health or psychosocial services, or patient case management**. Do not add **disaster response, relief distribution, shelters, rescue, WASH infrastructure, food delivery, cash transfers or humanitarian logistics**. Specialist emergency work relies on staff, systems, permissions and partners; Save the Children’s public emergency and health portfolio is evidence of the scope involved, not a model to claim without those foundations. [3]

Do not add **elder residential care, home care, pensions, legal aid, elder-abuse response, livelihood finance, jobs or placement services**. Do not add **animal rescue, veterinary treatment, animal sheltering, sterilisation or adoption**. Do not add **legal advice, RTI filing support, human-rights casework, certificate verification, accredited certification, or government/system-reform claims**.

Do not add a **school-meal, scholarship, nutrition or fee-support programme** unless the eligibility rules, selection, conflict controls, funding continuity, delivery permissions and evidence system exist. Akshaya Patra’s core model depends on a documented PM POSHAN implementation system, extensive kitchens and government partnerships; it cannot be represented by a donation appeal alone. [9]

Do not add an **app-first learning programme, public AI chatbot, AI assessment, digital-only course or online certificate**. Do not add a standalone **WASH programme** or water-infrastructure claim. charity: water’s work shows that even infrastructure support requires needs assessment, local technical partners, implementation capacity, maintenance and monitoring. [2]

## Evidence and language rules

For every completed activity, keep one internal record with the **claim, place, period, intended group, actual reach/attendance, activity, Abhiara role, partner role, source record, consent status, cost/support record where relevant, and feedback or observation.** Publish a safe, proportionate version of it. Use “participants attended,” “sessions were delivered,” or “materials were provided” for outputs. Reserve “improved,” “increased,” “reduced,” “enabled,” or “changed” for outcomes that have a stated method, date range, sample and limitation.

Use inclusion and safeguarding as delivery standards only when the relevant policy and practice are already adopted and usable. Do not infer outcomes from a participant story. Co-design and learner voice are good practices, but Plan International’s youth-centred approach and broad girls-rights programme are not evidence that Abhiara has comparable capacity or mandate. [10]

The benchmark is therefore not “more programmes.” It is **one well-evidenced education programme; a tightly bounded help/referral route; an honest archive of past classes; and clearly labelled future digital-learning and wellness ideas.** Add a new programme only after need, authority, staff or partner capability, safeguarding, funding, delivery model and measurement are in place.
