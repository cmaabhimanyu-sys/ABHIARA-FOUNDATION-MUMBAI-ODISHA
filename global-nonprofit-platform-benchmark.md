# Abhiara Foundation — Global Nonprofit Platform Benchmark and Practical Website Plan

**Purpose:** a small, warm, bilingual public platform that is credible at an early stage, protects people’s privacy, makes the current education purpose unmistakable, and retains a limited, reviewable historical record.

**Recommendation in one sentence:** Keep **`https://www.abhiarafoundation.org`** as the sole public domain; lead with one current education purpose; put limited verified support, historic records, and future elder care in clearly bounded secondary locations; and make every public statement traceable, dated, bilingual, privacy-reviewed, and easy to understand.

> **Operating principle:** *Education first. Compassion always.* A simple site with a few true, dated facts is more trustworthy than a broad programme catalogue, a gallery, or a large set of unsupported claims.

---

## 1. Non-negotiable platform standard

### What the site must preserve

| Decision | Public standard |
|---|---|
| **Official identity** | Every canonical URL, payment link, sitemap entry, social/OG URL, QR instruction, and public reference uses **`https://www.abhiarafoundation.org`**. Redirect the non-`www` host to the equivalent `www` URL; do not promote `.com` or personal-payment links. |
| **Primary purpose** | **Abhiara Shiksha Sathi** is the single primary active programme. Its page states only the current, independently verified education activity, intended learners, safe broad geography, approach, last-verified date, and modest evidence. |
| **Secondary scope** | Limited verified support is discoverable but subordinate: it is capacity-bound, eligibility/verification-based, and never described as a broad continuing portfolio. Historical support records must say they are historic and outside the flagship programme. |
| **Elder care** | **Abhiara Vidyapitha** is a future vision only: **not an operating elder home, not a current programme, and not evidence of an active elder-care service**. Its page may describe a conditional long-term aspiration, dependencies, and no current service availability. |
| **Language and tone** | Use short, warm, plain **English and Odia** with equivalent meaning. Human review—not automatic translation alone—is required for donation, tax, safeguarding, eligibility, privacy, and historical-record copy. |
| **Privacy** | No public child or founder private details. Default to text-led, non-identifying updates; no child face/voice, names, school/class plus locality combination, family circumstance, aid amount, routes, learner records, or sensitive “before/after” story. |
| **Giving** | One-time payment only. No recurring toggle, subscription, mandate, instalment, renewal language, monthly campaign, or post-payment recurring upsell. |
| **Tax and acknowledgement** | **80G approval is pending.** Do not claim a tax deduction or issue an “80G receipt.” Use **Donation Acknowledgement** wording only (approved copy below). |
| **Donors** | No donor wall, public donor list, public amount/reference, donor dashboard, sponsorship, or child-to-donor tracking. |
| **Claims** | No broad impact counters, “50+” fallback totals, scale statements, unsupported award/partner/security claims, or current-programme inference from historic activity. Every number needs a definition, time period, source, reviewer, and last-verified date. |
| **Programme presentation** | Do not use a cluttered strip of eight programme cards. There is one core education programme; records, plans, and limited support are separate content states—not equal programmes. |

This deliberately small structure applies the most useful common benchmark pattern: a visitor should be able to understand **the work now**, **the evidence and governance**, and **the one action available** without mistaking archives or plans for delivery.[1][2][3][4]

## 2. Recommended information architecture

### Primary navigation — five items only

`Home · Education · Updates · Transparency · Donate once`

Utility/header controls: **English | ଓଡ଼ିଆ**, Contact, and a single persistent **Donate once** button.

The language switch must preserve the equivalent page where possible. If a reviewed Odia translation is temporarily unavailable, show a visible, honest “Odia translation in review” label; do not leave donation, safeguarding, eligibility, or tax information in English only.

### Secondary/footer destinations

| Footer group | Destination | Role and required framing |
|---|---|---|
| **Understand** | **Our story / purpose** | Warm organisation-level purpose section, not a founder biography. |
|  | **Birthday with Purpose** | A quiet participation page that points to the single one-time education donation route; bounded design below. |
| **Records** | **Historical education archive** (inside Updates) | Two reviewed education records, visibly separate from current reporting. |
|  | **Past verified support** | Three bounded non-education records, clearly outside the flagship programme. |
|  | **Abhiara Vidyapitha** | Future vision—not a current programme or elder home. |
| **Trust** | **Board & transparency** | Legal identity/statuses, people/roles where verified, documents, financial-status note, and public-record method. |
|  | **Safeguarding, Privacy, Donation & Refund Policy** | Dated policies and accessible concerns/contact route. |
| **Help** | Contact / report a concern | Public email/contact and a safe, restricted safeguarding route. |

This is intentionally smaller than the mature organisations reviewed. Pratham makes current education, publications, leadership, legal information, and donation routes findable without requiring visitors to decode internal projects; SATHI’s current-versus-previous-project distinction is useful when reduced to Abhiara’s scale.[1][4]

### Content-state rule

Every public card, update, document, and plan must receive exactly one controlled status label:

1. **Current education work** — activity operating now; include last-verified date.
2. **Historical education archive** — completed/past education record; never a current monthly metric.
3. **Past verified support — outside current flagship programme** — bounded historic non-education record.
4. **Future vision — not a current programme** — a conditional plan, not a result or service offer.
5. **Draft / under review** — internal only; never public.

A clear distinction among current work, archive, and planned activity is a transferable reporting discipline found across the benchmarks; it avoids representing a future intention or one-off record as current delivery.[1][2][3][4]

## 3. Homepage plan: warm, evidence-led, and uncluttered

Use a calm, text-forward design with generous white space, a warm but restrained visual palette, and non-identifying imagery only where it has passed the media gate. The homepage must not contain a programme-carousel strip, legacy gallery, donor list, dramatic beneficiary case study, or broad counter.

| Order | Section | Required content and decision |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Header** | Logo; English/Odia switch; compact navigation; one button: **Donate once**. Use the official `www` domain in all action links. |
| 2 | **Education-first hero** | Plain, warm statement of present purpose: education continuity and dignity through Abhiara Shiksha Sathi. One primary CTA: **Explore our education work**; a secondary text link: **Make a one-time donation**. No unsupported number or photograph of an identifiable child. |
| 3 | **What we are doing now** | One concise current-education card: activity/approach, safe broad geography if approved, intended learner group, last verified date, and “What can be verified” link. If no current public metric is ready, state the date and activity without inventing one. |
| 4 | **How support is considered** | A short process explanation: education is first; limited verified support may be considered only within capacity and after verification. Link to the policy/secondary archive—do not create multiple programme cards. |
| 5 | **A current update or report** | One dated, privacy-safe education update: what happened, why it matters, what is verified, source/period, and next review. Link to Updates. Do not use historical archive activity as proof of current operations. |
| 6 | **Purpose, warmly restored** | A small **“Why this work matters”** / **“A purpose carried forward”** section. Write in an organisation-level voice about education, dignity, and careful help. No founder photograph, personal journey, family history, location trail, private quotation, qualification, or claim that a founder story proves impact. |
| 7 | **Birthday with Purpose invitation** | A calm card: “Mark a birthday with a one-time gift for education.” Link to the bounded page below. Do not show registries, fundraising totals, honouree data, sponsor tiers, e-cards, or social proof. |
| 8 | **Trust line** | A small factual line: “One-time donation only · 80G approval pending · Donation Acknowledgement available on request.” Link to Donate and Transparency. |
| 9 | **Trust-rich footer** | Legal/public-contact information only after validation; Board & transparency, policies, current reports, historical records, Vidyapitha future vision, and Contact. Keep archives in the footer/records area rather than primary programme navigation. |

The compact action → mission → evidence → trust journey is adapted from the useful underlying approach of charity: water, but not its visual language, funding model, claims, donor products, photography, maps, or campaign mechanics.[2]

## 4. Page-by-page content plan

### A. Education — the only primary programme page

**Public title:** `Abhiara Shiksha Sathi`  
**Purpose:** explain the current education activity without overselling it.

Required blocks:

1. **What is happening now** — simple description of verified education-continuity work.
2. **Who it is intended to help** — only the broad, approved audience description; no personal records or case profiles.
3. **How a request/support decision is considered** — factual process and safeguarding boundary, not an eligibility promise or direct child-referral funnel.
4. **What can be verified** — dated aggregate only if it has a period, definition, source, reviewer, and limitations.
5. **Latest education update** — link to a dated update; no unreviewed “success story.”
6. **Limits and next review** — say what data is still being collected or what has not been independently verified.
7. **Donate once** — one link to the one-time flow, with no child-specific allocation promise.

Do not place elder care, water, animal care, emergency relief, health, culture, or an eight-programme inventory on this page. The benchmarks support making active method and dated evidence findable in one place, not manufacturing a large programme taxonomy.[1][3]

### B. Updates — current reporting first, archive clearly below

At the top: **Current education reporting** only. Each update follows this fixed bilingual template:

1. **What we observed / what happened** — activity and safe broad place; month/period.
2. **What we did** — action and education purpose.
3. **What can be verified** — narrow aggregate/milestone, definition, source and whether provisional/final.
4. **What we learned / next review** — one modest action or review date, explicitly future-facing if not yet done.
5. **Privacy and verification note** — anonymisation applied, media status, and correction/removal contact.

Below a strong divider, show **Historical education archive** with the two reviewed record cards. Never call these “current monthly reports,” current beneficiaries, or cumulative programme reach.

#### Historical education records to restore after record-level approval

| Record title | Publicly bounded facts | Mandatory card language |
|---|---|---|
| **School books and dictionaries for students — 15 August 2026** | Books and dictionaries were distributed to students in Raisar, Kendrapara, to encourage education continuity. **No verified student count was recorded.** | **Historical verified education activity — not a current monthly delivery metric.** Use a text-only card unless rights, consent, safeguarding, and media review approve a specific asset. |
| **Abhiara Pratibha Samman — 4 June 2026** | **57 students** were honoured; legacy record describes trophies, certificates, and medals. State geography only at the approved district/block level. | **Historical verified education activity — not a current monthly delivery metric.** “57” is event-specific; it is not a cumulative/current student count. Cite/link a reviewed press source only after source/date/rights review. |

For either record, a missing primary source, unresolved date, media-rights question, or safeguarding decision means **withhold the detail, use a narrower text-only version, or do not publish**. This follows the benchmark distinction between current programme information, dated reports, and older archive material.[1][2][3][4]

### C. Past verified support — a secondary, bounded archive

**Location:** footer/Transparency/Records link; not a primary nav item, homepage programme card, or donation campaign.

Introductory copy should say:

> These are limited, dated historical records outside Abhiara Foundation’s current flagship education programme. They do not indicate a continuing service, geographic operating footprint, or general eligibility for support.

Show in reverse chronology, text-only by default:

| Record title | Publicly bounded facts | Mandatory limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Kankili fire-relief support — June 2026** | One family received an in-person damage check and immediate essential supplies. Use **June 2026** until the precise date discrepancy is resolved against a primary record. | **Historical — outside the current flagship programme; one-time, case-based support.** No family name, circumstances, aid amount, photo/video, or wider outcome claim. |
| **Pana Sankranti free-water service — 14 April 2026** | Free water service took place at Koraput, Kendrapara, and Bhubaneswar on the occasion. **No verified beneficiary count was recorded.** | **Historical — outside the current flagship programme.** No claim of a recurring water camp, current service, or area-wide operating presence. |
| **Elder-home visit — October 2025** | A one-time visit shared essentials and companionship with **40+ elder residents** in Puri, subject to final source and host/rights review. | **Historical one-time visit — not an elder-care programme or an old-age home operated by Abhiara.** Do not publish host/facility details, identifiable elder media, health/family/care-continuity claims, or a future-service promise. |

These records are evidence of limited historic action, not a reintroduction of the former Activities, Gallery, Impact, Media Room, or broad Blog experience. SATHI’s separate treatment of current and previous work is a useful model only when Abhiara’s archive labels are even more explicit.[4]

### D. Abhiara Vidyapitha — future vision page

This page is intentionally short and non-promotional.

**Required heading:** `Abhiara Vidyapitha — Future vision, not a current programme`  
**Required explanation:** dignified elder care is a long-term aspiration only. No elder home is currently operated by Abhiara Foundation.

Show only:

- the broad aspiration, in simple English/Odia;
- conditions before any service can begin: board approval, lawful registrations/permissions, safeguarding and operating model, suitable facility, staffing/partners, and confirmed resources;
- an honest status: **not started / no current service availability**;
- a link back to Education and Transparency.

Do **not** solicit dedicated gifts for an unapproved facility, accept referrals, imply beds or admissions, publish a launch forecast, or use the 2025 elder-home visit as evidence of operating readiness.

### E. Warm founder-purpose section / Our story

Restore purpose—not private biography.

**Suggested safe form:**

> **A purpose carried forward**  
> Abhiara Foundation exists to stand carefully beside learning, dignity, and everyday hope. We begin with education because steady learning can help children continue with confidence. We will speak plainly about what is happening now, what is still only a plan, and where help is limited.

This can be expanded slightly after an owner review, but it must remain organisation-led. A legally necessary director/officer disclosure belongs in Board & transparency, not in the story. No personal founder name beyond what lawfully belongs in governance disclosure, image, home/community history, qualifications, private hardships, family details, personal message, or broad claim of ongoing non-education delivery. A founder narrative must never be used as impact evidence.

### F. Birthday with Purpose — bounded participation page

**Goal:** preserve a warm occasion pathway without creating a new payment product, donor wall, peer-to-peer fundraiser, sponsorship model, or public campaign.

**Public title:** `Birthday with Purpose`  
**Simple proposition:** “If you wish, mark your birthday with a one-time gift for education.”

**Page structure:**

1. A short, warm explanation of choosing education as a birthday gesture.
2. One link/button: **Make a one-time donation for education** → the standard donation flow.
3. Optional, copyable private sharing text (English/Odia) that links only to `www.abhiarafoundation.org/donate`.
4. A concise assurance: no birthday registry, no public donor list, no minimum target, no personal campaign page, no child sponsorship, and no recurring donation.
5. Donation Acknowledgement and 80G-pending information, linked to the standard policy.

Do not ask for a birth date, recipient name, guest list, messages, photos, social account, fundraising goal, or occasion data unless a future privacy/legal review establishes a necessary, safe purpose. Do not create a separate checkout, e-card system, birthday counter, “gift in someone’s honour” product, or peer-to-peer/occasion fundraising route. The page is an editorial invitation to use the **existing one-time donation** method, not a different fundraising programme.

## 5. Donation, acknowledgement, privacy, and payment plan

### One short donation route

**CTA label:** `Donate once` / approved Odia equivalent  
**Donation label at checkout:** `One-time donation / ଏକକ ଦାନ`

Keep the journey to: amount (three/four modest suggestions plus Other amount) → minimum receipt/compliance details → separate optional communications consent, unticked → authorised processor → confirmation.

Before payment, display:

1. verified legal payee/entity name and payment method;
2. the purpose: support for Abhiara’s education-first work, subject to the current published policy;
3. payment-processor information and Privacy link;
4. receipt/acknowledgement timing and donor-help/refund contact;
5. the exact one-time and tax-status notices below;
6. official-domain anti-fraud notice.

Payment-card/bank credentials stay with the authorised processor. The public site/dashboard must not store them; payment and donor records stay separate from programme/child data. The existing technical assessment confirms the checkout accepts one-time payments only, while credential rotation and deployment-secret hygiene remain mandatory before a secure operational sign-off.[6]

### Required payment notices — use exact meaning in both languages

> **One-time donation only. No recurring charge, subscription, mandate, instalment, or automatic renewal is created.**

> **80G approval is pending. Current donations are not eligible for an 80G tax deduction.**

> **A Donation Acknowledgement may be requested. It confirms that a donation has been received; it is not an 80G certificate or tax receipt.**

> **Donate only through `www.abhiarafoundation.org`, the published Foundation account, or the authorised payment channel shown there. Abhiara Foundation will not change payment details through an unsolicited message.**

### Confirmation state

After a successful payment, show only: payment/reference identifier to the donor, amount, date, acknowledgement expectation, help/refund contact, and link to current education updates. Do not promise a named beneficiary, individual tracking, photograph, special report, tax receipt, or specific outcome.

The benchmark evidence supports clear pre-payment legal/data disclosures and separate privacy/marketing choices; it does **not** justify importing recurring models, tax claims, allocation promises, or large-scale donor products.[1][2][3][4]

## 6. Trust, transparency, safeguarding, and privacy standard

### One usable Transparency page

Build **Board & transparency** as one maintained page rather than several empty trust destinations. Publish only facts that are verified, appropriate to publish, and date-stamped:

- legal entity name, incorporation/registration information, registered office, official contact, and statutory governing roles **after independent validation**;
- Section 8/CIN, PAN, 12AB, 80G, FCRA, or other statutory status **only when factual and applicable**; say “pending,” “not applicable,” or “not yet publicly available” plainly rather than implying approval;
- board/director name, role, appointment month/year, one relevant non-sensitive context line, and conflict-of-interest statement/policy link;
- safeguarding policy, code of conduct, concern/complaints route, and response expectations;
- concise donor/form privacy notice, payment-processor handling, retention criteria, correction/deletion route subject to legal requirements, and communications-consent rule;
- annual activity/stewardship note, financial status/documents as available, and an honest “not yet available” status where documents are not ready;
- document period, type, source, publication date, last review date, and supersedes/archive status.

A small, dated, accessible disclosure set is more credible than empty pages or generic compliance badges. The reviewed Indian nonprofits make governance, financial documentation, privacy, and safeguarding discoverable; Abhiara should scale that pattern to evidence it can actually maintain.[1][3][4]

### Safeguarding and media gate

Default public update: **text only**. Before publishing any image, video, identifiable adult quote, press clipping, or record involving a child, elder, emergency-aid recipient, or other vulnerable person, require all of the following internal checks:

1. fact source and date reviewed;
2. privacy/minimisation assessment and safe location granularity;
3. documented, specific, revocable consent where applicable; for a child, guardian consent plus age-appropriate assent and safeguarding review;
4. dignity/public-interest, re-identification, metadata/geolocation, and context-risk assessment;
5. rights/licence and takedown-control decision;
6. English/Odia editorial review;
7. named approval owner and fixed review/expiry date.

A former public photo, legacy URL, storage asset, social post, newspaper clipping, or old consent is **not** automatic permission for new public use. If any check is incomplete, publish a narrower text-only record or withhold it. This is stricter than the personal child-story conventions seen in some benchmark material and deliberately so.[1][3][4]

### Safe update voice

Use an adult educator/community perspective or an aggregated programme observation where useful. Avoid child-centred transformation marketing. Never publish a child’s name, face, voice, exact school or village, course/class, attendance/learning record, address/route, household/family/health/trauma data, aid amount, or a re-identifying combination of otherwise ordinary details.

## 7. No-code management model

Use a small role-restricted owner dashboard. Its job is controlled publication—not a public impact portal or a datastore for sensitive records.

### Four public-content collections

| Collection | What it manages | Essential controlled fields |
|---|---|---|
| **Programmes** | The one current education programme and the Vidyapitha future-vision reference only. | title; purpose; status; start/target period; last verified date; evidence note; dependency; English/Odia completion; public/private; owner; review date. |
| **Updates** | Current education updates and all five historical records. | controlled public label; activity date/period; safe geography; verified facts; limitation; metric/definition; source/primary-record reference; fact reviewer; archival state; evidence-link decision; English/Odia copy; publish/review dates. |
| **Documents** | Policies, legal/public filings, stewardship notes, financial documents, and approved evidence references. | title; type; reporting period; source; issue/expiry; publication and review dates; public/private; English/Odia availability; superseded by; archive flag; file/link; approval owner. |
| **People & policies** | Board/role cards and policy summaries/contacts. | name only where public disclosure is approved; role; statutory/advisory/operating classification; appointment period; verified bio line; policy type/effective date; contact route; publication/review status. |

### Private operational registers (not public collections)

- **Donation ledger:** payment reference, amount, date, donor contact needed for acknowledgement/compliance, consent, payment/receipt/acknowledgement/reconciliation status, restricted access. No card/bank credentials and no child/programme case data.
- **Safeguarding/consent and media-review register:** internal consent reference, anonymisation decision, rights/asset decision, reviewer, expiry/removal request. Keep complaints/case attachments separate and accessible only to authorised safeguarding roles.
- **Site settings:** canonical domain, verified payee/processor display, official contact, bilingual payment/legal copy version, navigation, and a published “last verified” date.

### Publication workflow (must block publishing)

A record cannot publish until the owner completes: fact source → verification/date decision → status label → privacy/safeguarding and media gate → rights decision → English/Odia equivalence → responsible approval → publish date and next review date. Every edit should retain a small change log. Corrections append a dated note; do not silently rewrite a past claim.

This adapts the benchmark preference for structured status, period, source, language, privacy review, and archive fields while keeping the public operation manageable.[1][2][3][4]

## 8. Do not add

- An eight-programme band, multi-sector portfolio, large geography filter, or programmes presented as co-equal with education.
- A current elder-care/old-age-home claim, admissions/referral route, or future-vision fundraising pitch for Abhiara Vidyapitha.
- Recurring/monthly donation controls, subscription/mandate language, recurring FAQ, auto-renewal, donation frequency selector, or post-payment monthly ask.
- Public donor names, donor wall, donation amount/reference, donor dashboard, child sponsorship, individual gift tracking, or a “give to a named child” promise.
- Tax receipt/80G eligibility claim while approval remains pending; do not rename a Donation Acknowledgement as a receipt.
- Broad “impact” totals, fallback counters, unverified active-support figures, per-rupee impact mappings, “100%” claims, generic secure-payment seals, awards, ratings, or partner logos without documentary basis and permission.
- A photo gallery, legacy Media Room, broad Blog, legacy Activities/Impact landing pages, old videos, unreviewed press scans, or child/elder/emergency-aid imagery used for persuasion.
- Founder biography, private founder story, private photos or quotes, or personal details used as charity marketing.
- Any future plan shown beside delivered results without the **Future vision — not a current programme** label and dependencies.
- Birthday registry, public campaign page, goal tracker, peer-to-peer fundraiser, e-cards, gift-in-honour workflow, guest data collection, or occasion checkout.
- Unreviewed Odia auto-translation, personally identifying stories, or a publishing workflow that bypasses safeguarding and evidence review.

## 9. Prioritised implementation sequence and release gates

### Priority 0 — protect truth, privacy, and the official payment route

1. Enforce `www` canonical/redirect behaviour across routing, sitemaps, structured data, forms, QR/payment instructions, and social metadata.
2. Remove/disable stale broad counts, legacy programme signals, recurring remnants, public donor functions, and unreviewed media/asset paths.
3. Verify legal payee, processor configuration, one-time-only implementation, payment descriptor, official account/QR details, and public contact; rotate previously exposed credentials and use deployment secrets only.[6]
4. Apply the exact 80G-pending, Donation Acknowledgement, one-time-only, and anti-fraud wording in English and Odia at every payment surface.
5. Establish private media/consent/right and record-source inventories before publishing any historical content.

### Priority 1 — establish the small public foundation

1. Publish/review the five-item navigation and compact education-first homepage.
2. Build Education, Updates, Board & transparency, Donate once, and the bilingual policy/contact pages.
3. Add the controlled status model and no-code publishing gate.
4. Restore the safe organisation-level purpose section and the bounded Birthday with Purpose page.
5. Add the short Vidyapitha future-vision page with its explicit non-operational disclaimer.

### Priority 2 — publish only reviewed historical evidence

Publish in this order after each individual record clears the go/no-go test:

1. **Abhiara Pratibha Samman** — text-only, event-specific `57 students`, source/evidence review.
2. **15 August education/books** — text-only, no student count.
3. **Kankili fire-relief support** — text-only, `June 2026` until date reconciles.
4. **Pana Sankranti free-water service** — text-only, three locations and no beneficiary count.
5. **Elder-home visit** — text-only, one-time/historical and not an elder-care programme.

Create useful, neutral archive-review destinations for legacy URLs before redirecting them to individual cards. Keep archive routes out of primary navigation and marketing SEO.

### Priority 3 — operate and maintain

1. Publish a short quarterly **What we did / What we learned / What can be verified** education update only when a record is ready.
2. At year end, publish an appropriately scoped Activity & stewardship note and financial-status/document update; do not simulate a mature annual report.
3. Re-review public policies, statutory/payment copy, records, translations, links, and media at fixed dates and whenever the underlying fact changes.
4. Test mobile/desktop routes, old URLs/anchors, `www` canonicalisation, forms, checkout one-time behaviour, payment confirmation, receipt/acknowledgement flow, sitemap, and bilingual equivalence before every release.
5. Maintain regression checks for forbidden claims/features: non-`www` public links, recurring/subscription wording, 80G/tax receipt language, donor wall/dashboard, unsupported counters, and unreviewed legacy galleries/media.

### Record publication go/no-go test

A record is publishable only when every answer is **yes**:

- Is each public fact traceable to a primary or otherwise verified source?
- Is the date reconciled, or deliberately reduced to a safe month/period?
- Is each count bounded to the event, defined, and limited where needed?
- Does it carry the correct historical/non-flagship/future status?
- Have child, family, founder, donor, elder, and aid-recipient private details been removed?
- Is media absent or specifically consent-reviewed, safeguarding-approved, rights-cleared, and technically controllable?
- Is a press reference attributed and permitted for the chosen use?
- Do English and Odia say the same thing?
- Does the `www` canonical/redirect route lead to a useful, non-misleading destination?
- Has a responsible owner approved publication and next review date?

**Any “no” means do not publish, publish a narrower text-only record, or keep it internal.**

## 10. Benchmark rationale and citation notes

This plan adopts **patterns**, not any organisation’s copy, visual identity, programme names, photographs, stories, data, funding claims, donor products, seals, maps, or national/international scale signals.

- **Pratham** demonstrates that education, governance, legal/financial information, reports, and donation can remain findable through a clear structure; its sizeable programme taxonomy and identifiable personal storytelling are not transferable to Abhiara.[1]
- **charity: water** demonstrates a useful separation among action, work, formal reporting, stories, and privacy disclosures; its recurring giving, sponsorship, 100% model, donor/project tracking, visual system, and water-specific mechanics are not transferable.[2]
- **Bal Raksha Bharat / Save the Children India** demonstrates visible safeguarding, privacy, governance, and financial destinations, plus dated archives; its broad portfolio, large-scale claims, recurring donation model, and child-identifying story detail are not transferable.[3]
- **SATHI India** demonstrates clear current-versus-previous project treatment and annual report retention; its multi-programme structure, public individual case details, cumulative counters, and fundraising patterns are not transferable.[4]
- Abhiara’s record names, limitations, payment wording, official-domain direction, and privacy boundaries come from the supplied internal restoration/domain/donation/payment audits; historic items remain subject to source, consent, safeguarding, and rights approval before public release.[5][6][7][8]
