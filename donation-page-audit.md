# Donation Page Audit

## Abhiara Foundation live page

The current page is visually strong but too long. The donor meets a hero, a story block, the payment form, UPI and bank transfer, a second impact section, FAQ, memorial giving, occasion giving, compliance notices, another founder quote, contact links, and the full footer. The main form also shows an inactive Monthly Support choice, an NRI checkbox, five cause choices, ten optional designation choices, and a two-step Continue action before payment.

The useful parts are the one-time amount choices, cause selection, donor details, anonymous amount preference, Razorpay payment button, direct UPI QR, bank transfer details, 80G pending notice, and short FAQ. The repeated impact statements, secondary quote blocks, inactive monthly option, detailed gift-designation list, memorial and occasion promotions, and duplicated compliance information make the page feel heavy.

## CRY donation page

Source: https://www.cry.org/donation/

CRY presents a clear hero and places the donation form high on the page. It asks for citizenship, frequency, amount, identity, address, PAN, and declaration information. It has strong trust language and valid 80G claims, but the page is also long. The useful pattern for Abhiara is a direct amount-to-details flow. Abhiara should not copy CRY's 80G language because Abhiara's approval is still under process.

## Bal Raksha Bharat donation page

Source: https://balrakshabharat.org/donate/

Bal Raksha Bharat uses a simple split layout. A real programme image and short explanation appear on the left, while amount selection and the payment action appear on the right. Each selected amount has one clear impact sentence. The strongest lesson for Abhiara is to place the amount, impact, and payment action together instead of repeating impact information later on the page.

## Akshaya Patra online donation page

Source: https://www.akshayapatra.org/onlinedonations

Akshaya Patra begins with citizenship, donation type, amount, and one amount-to-impact sentence, then collects personal details. It separates special occasion, memorial, bank transfer, school, and kitchen giving into other navigation choices instead of repeating every option inside the main donation journey. Abhiara should keep memorial and occasion giving as links elsewhere, not large blocks inside the primary donation page.

## Proposed Abhiara structure

The page should have a compact hero, followed immediately by one donation panel. The panel should contain one-time amount choices, a short impact sentence, a short cause choice, donor details, optional PAN, the anonymous amount option, consent, and one Pay Securely button. Direct UPI and bank transfer can appear in one compact alternative-payment section below. A short three-question FAQ and a single compliance line can close the page before the footer.
