# NGO Website Research — Best Clean Designs for Education Foundations

## 1. Pratham (pratham.org) — BEST FOR EDUCATION
- Clean, minimal white design
- Simple nav: About Us, Programs, Get Involved, Resources, Contact, DONATE
- Programs organized clearly: Education > Early Years, Elementary, Hamara Gaon, Inclusive Education
- Separate "Stories of Change" section for real impact stories
- Financials page publicly available (transparency)
- Board and Leadership pages separate
- ASER (Annual Status of Education Report) — their own research publication
- Very text-focused, not image-heavy
- **Score: 9/10 for cleanliness**

## 2. Smile Foundation (smilefoundationindia.org) — BEST FOR MULTI-CAUSE
- Bold hero with current disaster relief (Assam Flood Relief)
- "Support A Cause" CTA prominent
- Campaigns as separate section: Shiksha Na Ruke, Health Cannot Wait, She Can Fly
- Partners carousel showing CSR partners
- Awards and recognition section builds trust
- Newsletter signup in footer
- Green brand color, energetic feel
- **Score: 7/10 — slightly busy but effective**

## 3. CRY - Child Rights and You (cry.org) — BEST FOR CHILD FOCUS
- Focus areas clearly listed: Education, Health, Stop Child Labour, Girl Child, Stop Child Marriage, Prevent Trafficking
- FAQ section on homepage (good for SEO and trust)
- Multiple donation methods: cards, UPI, wallets, legacy giving
- Monthly donor option prominent
- Simple, direct language throughout
- **Score: 8/10 for clarity**

## Key Takeaways for Abhiara Foundation
1. **Keep it simple** — Pratham's clean white design is the gold standard
2. **Lead with current work** — Smile Foundation puts latest disaster relief front and center
3. **Show financials publicly** — builds trust (Pratham does this well)
4. **Name your campaigns** — "Shiksha Sathi" is good, consider naming elderly care and disaster relief campaigns too
5. **FAQ on homepage** — CRY does this, good for donors who have questions
6. **Stories of Change** — real beneficiary stories (when available) are powerful
7. **Awards/Recognition** — if you get any, display them prominently
8. **Monthly giving** — make recurring donations easy and prominent
